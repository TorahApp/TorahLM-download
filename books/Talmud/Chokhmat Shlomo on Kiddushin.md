# Chokhmat Shlomo on Kiddushin

חכמת שלמה על קידושין

Link: [https://torahapp.org/share/book/Chokhmat%20Shlomo%20on%20Kiddushin](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Chokhmat%20Shlomo%20on%20Kiddushin)

16th-century glosses by the Maharshal focused on establishing the correct text of the Talmud.
