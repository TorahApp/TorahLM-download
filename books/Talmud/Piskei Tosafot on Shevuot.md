# Piskei Tosafot on Shevuot

פסקי תוספות על שבועות

Link: [https://torahapp.org/share/book/Piskei%20Tosafot%20on%20Shevuot](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Piskei%20Tosafot%20on%20Shevuot)
