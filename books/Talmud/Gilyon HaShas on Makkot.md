# Gilyon HaShas on Makkot

גליון הש"ס על מכות

Link: [https://torahapp.org/share/book/Gilyon%20HaShas%20on%20Makkot](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Gilyon%20HaShas%20on%20Makkot)
