# Maharam on Pesachim

מהר"ם על פסחים

Link: [https://torahapp.org/share/book/Maharam%20on%20Pesachim](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Maharam%20on%20Pesachim)

17th-century work by Rabbi Meir of Lublin with concise interpretations of the talmudic text and the commentaries of Rashi and Tosafot.
