## Chapter 1

###### Tractate Tefillin 1:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Tefillin/r/1:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Tefillin/r/1:1)


אין כותבין תפילין לא על עורות בהמה טמאה ולא על עורות חיה טמאה אבל כותבין על עורות נבלות וטרפות ואין חוששין שמא עורות לבובין הן:


It is not permitted to write [the four scrolls of] *tefillin*¹ on skins of ritually unclean cattle or on skins of ritually unclean wild animals. It is permitted, however, to write them on skins of *nebeloth* and *ṭerefoth* and there is no need to consider the possibility of their having been pierced at the animal’s heart.²

footnotes:
¹ These are specified below. Cf. also the Introduction.
² There was a heathen practice to remove the heart from an animal while it was still alive and offer it as a sacrifice. Rabbinic law regards such an animal as an idolatrous offering (cf. Mishnah ‘A.Z. 29b, Sonc. ed., p. 145) and forbids its entire use, including the skin.


###### Tractate Tefillin 1:2

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Tefillin/r/1:2](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Tefillin/r/1:2)


נקבו במקום הכתב פסולים במקום הריוח כשרים עשאן ווין זיינין זיינין ווין פשוטים כפופים כפופים פשוטים פתוחים סתומים סתומים פתוחים או ששנה אותיותיהן או שכתבן עברית או שעשאן כמין קמיע הרי אלו פסולים:


If [*tefillin* scrolls] were perforated, they become unfit for religious use [if the hole occurred] in the written part; but if it occurred in the margin they remain fit. If the letter *waw* was shaped like a *zayin* or *vice versa*, straight-lined letters like curved ones³, e.g., like an ordinary *kaf.* or *vice versa*, open letters closed⁴ like a final *mem.* or *vice versa*, or if the shape of their letters had been altered [in any other way], or if they were written in [obsolete] Hebrew, or were made as a kind of amulet—they are unfit for religious use.

footnotes:
³ The final kaf
⁴ Such as an ordinary mem


###### Tractate Tefillin 1:3

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Tefillin/r/1:3](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Tefillin/r/1:3)


נשים ועבדים וקטנים פטורין מן התפילין כל קטן שהוא יודע לשמור את תפיליו חייב בתפילין מעשה בטבי עבדו של רבי גמליאל שהיה מניח תפילין מיכל בת שאול היתה מנחת תפילין אשתו של יונה היתה עולה לירושלם:


Women, slaves and minors⁵ §1. are exempt from [the commandment of] *tefillin*. A minor who knows how to take care of his *tefillin* is subject to the law of *tefillin*. It is related that Ṭabi, R. Gamaliel’s slave, used to put on *tefillin;* Michal, Saul’s daughter, used to put on *tefillin;* Jonah’s wife used to make the festival pilgrimages⁶ to Jerusalem.⁷

footnotes:
⁵ Boys under thirteen years of age. On the exemption of women, cf. Ẓiẓith
⁶ [For Ṭabi, cf. j.‘Erub. X, I, 26a; for the two women, cf. ‘Erub. 96a (Sonc. ed., p. 665).]
⁷ Although the commandment applied only to males (cf. Ex. 34, 23).


###### Tractate Tefillin 1:4

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Tefillin/r/1:4](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Tefillin/r/1:4)


נושאי המטה וחילופיהן וחילופי חילופיהן (או) [את] שלפני המטה (או) [את] שלאחר המטה כל שלצורך המטה פטורין ושאין צורך המטה בהם חייבין עמדו להספד המספידין פטורים והרואים את הפנים חייבין:


[Regarding] the bearers of a bier [to the burial place] and those who relieve them⁸ and those who relieve the latter, whether in front of the bier or behind the bier⁹—all who are required [to carry] the bier are exempt¹⁰). [from putting on *tefillin*], but those who are not required for the bier are liable. When a halt is made for lamentations,¹¹ those who are engaged in the lamentations are exempt, but those¹² who cannot see¹³ the inside [space between the rows]¹⁴ are liable.

footnotes:
⁸ In carrying the bier.
⁹ Who have not yet carried it or have done so respectively.
¹⁰ This is in disagreement with the ruling in the Mishnah (Ber. 17b, Sonc. ed., p. 106) where only those who are in front of the bier are exempt if they are required to carry it. Those behind are always liable even if their help is required, since they had already done their duty to the dead (cf. Rashi ad loc.
¹¹ A number of such halts were made by those who returned from a burial to the mourner’s house. (Cf. Semaḥoth X.]
¹² In the back rows when they stand several deep.
¹³ So in accordance with Ber. 19b (Sonc. ed., p. 117). V reads ‘those who see’.
¹⁴ Where the mourner is.


###### Tractate Tefillin 1:5

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Tefillin/r/1:5](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Tefillin/r/1:5)


האומר בא על פני שהוא כתב או נתן עליו ספר נתן עליו תפילין ה״ז לא ישמע לו:


If a person says [to another], ‘Come and read [a Scriptural text] over my son because he is frightened’, or ‘Write, or place, upon him a [holy] scroll’, or ‘Put *tefillin* upon him that he may sleep’,¹⁵ he should not be listened to.¹⁶

footnotes:
¹⁵ This is the reading in j.Shab. VI, 2, 8b. The text in V is corrupt.
¹⁶ Because sacred objects should not be used for such purposes. Cf. Sanh. 101a (Sonc. ed., p. 684, nn. 6f.).


###### Tractate Tefillin 1:6

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Tefillin/r/1:6](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Tefillin/r/1:6)


(ש)התפילין מקבלת טומאה מזה ושונה ומטביל רבי חנינא בן גמליאל אומר צריך הוא להתירה נפלה לתוך המים ושהתה כדי שתמחק צריכה בדיקה:


A *tefillah*¹⁷ is susceptible of ritual uncleanness. [If it becomes unclean, the priest] sprinkles upon it [the water of the red heifer]¹⁸ and repeats the process, and then immerses it [in a ritual bath]. R. Ḥanina b. Gamaliel says: It is necessary to unloose it.¹⁹ If [a *tefillah*] fell into water and remained there long enough [for the writing] to be washed away, it should be subjected to an examination.²⁰

footnotes:
¹⁷ V has the plural form.
¹⁸ Cf. Num. 19.
¹⁹ To remove the stitches that hold the parts of the capsule together, in order that the water may penetrate to all parts.
²⁰ To make sure that the writing is still in a good condition or to repair it if necessary.


###### Tractate Tefillin 1:7

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Tefillin/r/1:7](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Tefillin/r/1:7)


רצועות תפילין לוקחין מכל מקום רשב״ג אומר כל עור שנעשה בו מלאכה אסור:


Straps of *tefillin* may be bought anywhere.²¹, to buy them from a reliable person. R. Simeon b. Gamaliel says: Any skin that had been used [for a secular] purpose is forbidden [to be used for the straps of *tefillin*].²²

footnotes:
²¹ There is no need, as in the case with the tefillin
²² Hence they must be bought only from a reliable person who can guarantee that no previous use had been made of the skin.


###### Tractate Tefillin 1:8

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Tefillin/r/1:8](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Tefillin/r/1:8)


אין לוקחין תפילין אלא מן המומחה ואם אין שם מומחה נותן זוג אחד ובודק לא שיתן לו פרשיות המוכר אלא שיטול הוא לעצמו ויבדוק בדק ומצא כראוי נוטל כל האגודה ואינו חושש:


*Tefillin* may be bought only from a reliable person;²³ and where there is no reliable person a pair of *tefillin* should be given [to an expert] who examines them. This does not mean that the seller gives him the scrolls, but that he himself chooses what he pleases and has them examined.²⁴ If on examination the selected *tefillin* prove to be fit,²⁵ he may take all the batch without any scruples.²⁶

footnotes:
²³ One who can testify that they were prepared in accordance with the law.
²⁴ lit. ‘that he takes for himself and he examines’.
²⁵ lit. ‘he examined and found as proper’.
²⁶ The fitness of the sample is assumed to represent the fitness of the whole batch.


###### Tractate Tefillin 1:9

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Tefillin/r/1:9](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Tefillin/r/1:9)


תפלה של ראש נכתבת ארבע פרשיות קדש לי והיה כי יביאך שמע והיה אם שמוע וכן של יד אלא שהוא עושה של יד פרשה אחת ואם עשאה ארבע פרשיות כשרה רבי יהודה אומר יתפור ויתנם לתוך קמיע מודה ר׳ יהודה שאם היה שניהם של ראש חופה אחת מהן (כעודן) [בעור] ועושה אותה של יד:


The [sections of the] head-*tefillah* are written on four separate scrolls²⁷ [beginning with] *Sanctify unto Me;*²⁸ *And it shall be when the Lord shall bring thee;*²⁹ 11-16. *Hear;*³⁰ *And it shall come to pass, if ye shall hearken*.³¹ XI, 13-21. So also with the hand-*tefillah*, except that [all the four sections] are written in one scroll.³² If one wrote them in four separate scrolls [the *tefillah*] is fit for religious use. R. Judah says: They should be sewn together and put into one capsule.³³, which consists of four compartments joined together to form one capsule. R. Judah, however, admits that if two *tefillin*³⁴ were [like] the head-*tefillah*, one may wrap one of them in a piece of leather and so convert it into a hand-*tefillah*.

footnotes:
²⁷ lit. ‘sections’.
²⁸ Ex. 13, 1-10.
²⁹ ibid.
³⁰ Deut. 6, 4-9.
³¹ ibid.
³² lit. ‘he makes one section’.
³³ Unlike the head-tefillah
³⁴ lit. ‘both of them’.


###### Tractate Tefillin 1:10

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Tefillin/r/1:10](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Tefillin/r/1:10)


תפלה של יד ניתנה בגובה של יד אף בין עיניך בגובה של ראש על ידך בין עיניך מה על ידך בגובה של יד אף בין עיניך בגובה של ראש כדברי ר״א בן יעקב ר׳ יהודה אומר נאמר על ידך ונאמר בין עיניך מה על ידך שהוא ראוי ליטמא (ברגע) [בנגע] אחד אף בין עיניך מקום שהוא ראוי ליטמא (ברגע) [בנגע] אחד נאמר בין עיניך ונאמר (דברים י״ד:א׳) ולא תשימו קרחה בין עיניכם מה קרחה במקום השער אף תפלה לא תתן אלא במקום השיער נאמר וכתבתם ונאמר וקשרתם יד הכותבת היא יד הקושרת:


The hand-*tefillah* is put on the upper part of the hand³⁵ [because Scripture declares, *And it shall be for a sign unto thee upon thy hand*,³⁶ which means that the sign shall be for thee³⁷ can see it, i.e. on the biceps covered by the sleeve. but not for others³⁸ been put on the palm of the hand it would be visible to all.]. ³⁹So also [the Scriptural expression] *between thine eyes*⁴⁰ [implies] the upper part of the head, [because] *upon thy hand* and *between thine eyes* [occur in juxtaposition we infer]: as *upon thy hand* [means] on the upper part of the hand, so *between thine eyes* [means] on the upper part of the head. This is according to R. Eliezer b. Jacob; R. Judah says: It is stated *upon thy hand* and it is also stated [in the same verse] *between thine eyes* [the inference being that] as *upon thy hand* [refers to] a place that is susceptible of ritual uncleanness by one [symptom of] leprosy,⁴¹ so also *between thine eyes* [refers to] a place that is susceptible of ritual uncleanness by one [symptom of] leprosy.⁴² [Another reason:] It is stated *between thine eyes* and it is also stated, *Nor make any baldness between your eyes:*⁴³ as baldness occurs where there is hair [on the head] so you must put the *tefillah* only where there is hair [on the hand].⁴⁴. It is stated, *And thou shalt write them*⁴⁵ (Deut. 6, 9). and it is also stated [in juxtaposition] *and thou shalt bind them;*⁴⁶ (*ibid.* 8). [hence the inference that] the hand that writes [the *mezuzah*]⁴⁷ is the hand that binds [the *tefillah*].⁴⁸ is therefore put on the left hand.

footnotes:
³⁵ The biceps, and not on the palm of the hand.
³⁶ Ex. 13, 9.
³⁷ Where only he who wears the tefillah
³⁸ Had the tefillah
³⁹ The part enclosed in square brackets is based on Men. 37b (Sonc. ed., p. 229).
⁴⁰ Ex. 13, 9.
⁴¹ The biceps, like any other part of the body that is free from hair, is declared ritually unclean by one symptom only, viz. white hair in the leprous spot.
⁴² Viz. the upper part of the head, whose symptom of uncleanness is yellow hair in the leprous spot. The region between the eyes, however, where both hair and clean flesh are to be found, can be declared unclean by either of the two symptoms (yellow or white hair) and must therefore be ruled out.
⁴³ Deut. 14, 1.
⁴⁴ Since both verses contain the expression between … eyes
⁴⁵ Referring to the mezuzah
⁴⁶ The tefillin
⁴⁷ i.e. the right hand, with which most people write.
⁴⁸ The hand-tefillah


###### Tractate Tefillin 1:11

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Tefillin/r/1:11](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Tefillin/r/1:11)


מצות תפילין מהשחר עד הערב הישן ביום אינו חושש לחלוץ את תפילין (המתכנין) [המשכים] לילך בדרך לא יתננה קודם למצוה ואם נותנה קודם למצוה נותן ידו עליהם בשעת מצוה ומברך:


The commandment of [wearing] *tefillin* is from dawn until evening. If a person sleeps in the day-time he need not trouble to remove his *tefillin*. If a man rises early to go on a journey, he should not put them on before the time [for the performance] of the commandment; but if he had put them on before [the proper time for the performance of] the commandment, he should place his hand upon them at the time [for the performance] of the commandment⁴⁹ and say the benedictions.

footnotes:
⁴⁹ At dawn.


###### Tractate Tefillin 1:12

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Tefillin/r/1:12](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Tefillin/r/1:12)


תפלה של יד אינה מעכבת של ראש ושל ראש אינה מעכבת של יד אם הפך את הקמיע מלמעלה לא יצא כשהוא נותן נותן של יד תחילה וכשהוא חולץ חולץ של ראש תחלה וכן לענין הסנדלין כשהוא נועל נועל של ימין תחלה וכשהוא חולץ חולץ של שמאל תחלה כיוצא בו כל הנכנס לסעודה (ראשון) [אחרון] יוצא ראשון חוץ מן הפת:


[Absence of] the hand-*tefillah* does not prevent the religious use of the head-*tefillah*, neither does [the absence of] the head-*tefillah* prevent the religious use of the hand-*tefillah*.⁵⁰ is independent of the other. If a man inverted a *tefillah* capsule⁵¹ he has not fulfilled his religious duty.
When a man puts on [the *tefillin*], he should put on that of the hand first; and when he takes them off, he should take off that of the head first. A similar rule applies in the case of sandals, viz. when he puts them on, he should put on that of the right foot first, and when he takes them off, he should take off that of the left foot first.⁵² Likewise, any [dish] that is served last⁵³ is removed first, except bread.⁵⁴

footnotes:
⁵⁰ Each tefillah
⁵¹ When it is on his hand or head.
⁵² [Cf. DER. X, 1.]
⁵³ Another and better reading is ‘first’.
⁵⁴ Which must remain on the table until the Grace after meals had been recited (cf. Sanh. 92a, Sonc. ed., p. 617).


###### Tractate Tefillin 1:13

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Tefillin/r/1:13](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Tefillin/r/1:13)


העושה תפלתו עגולה סכנה שאין בה מצוה נותנה על מצחו או על פיסת ידו הרי זו דרך המינות ציפה זהב ונותנה על נקלה שלו והוא בית יד הרי זו דרך החיצונים בית:


If a man makes his head-[*tefillah*] round, this is a dangerous practice⁵⁵ which does not carry with it [the fulfilment of] the commandment.⁵⁶ To put [the head-*tefillah*] on one’s forehead or [the hand-*tefillah*] on the palm of one’s hand⁵⁷ (Deut. 6, 8), which is contrary to the traditional interpretation. is in the manner of heresy.⁵⁸, used especially of the views and practices of the early Jewish-Christians. According to Maimonides the *minim* were Sadducees. To overlay them with gold or put [that of the hand] on one’s *neḳlah*, which means a sleeve, is the manner of the outsiders.⁵⁹, pp. 128f.] [63*a*]

footnotes:
⁵⁵ Because an accidental blow on it might split one’s head. Cf. Meg. 24b (Sonc. ed., p. 148, n. 2).
⁵⁶ The commandment being that it must be cube-shaped and flat at the base.
⁵⁷ In accordance with the literal meaning of the verse, And thou shalt bind them for a sign upon thy hand, and they shall be for frontlets between thine eyes
⁵⁸ Heb. minuth
⁵⁹ Persons who follow their own interpretation of Scripture and not that of the Rabbis. [On this paragraph, cf. J. Rabbinowitz, Mishnah Megillah


###### Tractate Tefillin 1:14

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Tefillin/r/1:14](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Tefillin/r/1:14)


מטלטלין תפילין בתוך הבית המוצא תפילין ברשות הרבים מכניסן זוג זוג ר״ג אומר שני זוגות אחד איש ואחד אשה אחד חדשות ואחד ישנות ר׳ יהודה אומר מותר בישנות ואסור בחדשות:


*Tefillin* may be moved within a house [even on the Sabbath].⁶⁰ If [on the Sabbath day] a man finds *tefillin* in a public domain,⁶¹ he should carry them [into a safe place] one pair at a time.⁶² on his arm and the head-*tefillah* on his head in the same manner as they are worn on week-days. R. Gamaliel says: Two pairs at a time.⁶³ on his arm and two head-*tefillin* on his head. [This rule applies to] both men and women,⁶⁴. to new *tefillin* and to old. R. Judah says: [This procedure] is permitted with old *tefillin*⁶⁵. but forbidden with new.⁶⁶

footnotes:
⁶⁰ They are not regarded as articles forbidden to be handled on the Sabbath or Festival, although not worn on the holy days.
⁶¹ Where they may be trodden upon.
⁶² The hand-tefillah
⁶³ As there is sufficient room on the arm and on the head for two sets, he may put two hand-tefillin
⁶⁴ Although the latter are exempt from the commandment of tefillin
⁶⁵ Which show marks of wear or have a proper knot, so that there can be no doubt that they were genuine tefillin
⁶⁶ Since they may be only amulets.


###### Tractate Tefillin 1:15

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Tefillin/r/1:15](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Tefillin/r/1:15)


המוצא ספר או תכריך של תפילין נותנם לחבירו וחבירו לחבירו בשבת ואפילו הם מאה אם עמו נוטלו אחד מתוך ארבע אמות ונותנו לתוך ארבע אמות שהיה ר׳ שמעון אומר כל שהוא עומד בפני ב״ד אינו עומד ולא בכתבי הקדש מי שהיה בבית הספר או בבית המדרש בבית הכסא או בבית המרחץ ושכח לחלוץ את תפילין מכסן בשערו ויוצא ואם אינו יכול נכנס לבית קרוב וחולצן:


If a man finds a [Torah] scroll or a bundle of *tefillin*,⁶⁷ he should pass them on to his fellow, and his fellow should pass them on to his fellow on the Sabbath,⁶⁸ even though they be a hundred persons. If [there is no other person]⁶⁹ with him, he⁷⁰ should take them up [while standing] within four cubits [distance from them] and remove them to [the next point] within four cubits [distance from the first point];⁷¹ because R. Simeon said: Any prohibition⁷² with H instead of *‘omed* (standing) in V. imposed by a Rabbinical Court⁷³ does not apply to holy writings.⁷⁴ to a place of safety, provided all the stages between the points of taking up and setting down are each less than four cubits in length.
If a man is in a school house or in a House of Study,⁷⁵ would not be sufficiently protected if left there. [or] in a privy or a bath-house,⁷⁶ to be left there. and forgot to remove his *tefillin*,⁷⁷ he should cover them with his hair⁷⁸ on the Sabbath in public. and leave the place.⁷⁹ If he is unable to do so, he should proceed to a near-by house where he takes them off.⁸⁰

footnotes:
⁶⁷ In a public domain where they may be trodden upon.
⁶⁸ Each one carrying them a lesser distance than four cubits. Carrying an article a longer distance in a public domain is forbidden on the Sabbath.

Beloved are Israel [by God], for they observe all the commandments that are in the Torah. [The Sages] illustrated this with a parable. To what may this be compared? Similarly the Holy One, blessed be He, said to Israel, ‘My children, be well dressed and adorned in My presence with [your performance of] the commandments’. ‘For what reason?’ they asked Him. ‘In order,’ He replied, ‘that you may be acceptable to Me’; as it is stated, *Thou art beautiful, O my love, as Tirẓah*,⁹⁸ [which means,] ‘Thou art beautiful when thou art acceptable⁹⁹ is of the same root as *raẓuï* (acceptable). to Me through [thy performance of] the commandments’.
