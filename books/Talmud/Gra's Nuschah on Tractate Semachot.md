Link: [https://torahapp.org/share/book/Gra%27s%20Nuschah%20on%20Tractate%20Semachot](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Gra%27s%20Nuschah%20on%20Tractate%20Semachot)

Generations of rabbinic debate about law, ethics, and Bible, structured as commentary on the Mishnah with stories interwoven.
