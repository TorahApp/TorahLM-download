# Penei Moshe on Jerusalem Talmud Makkot

Link: [https://torahapp.org/share/book/Penei%20Moshe%20on%20Jerusalem%20Talmud%20Makkot](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Penei%20Moshe%20on%20Jerusalem%20Talmud%20Makkot)

18th-century explanatory commentary by Rabbi Moshe Margalit and the earliest known commentary on the entire Jerusalem Talmud.
