# Chokhmat Shlomo on Gittin

חכמת שלמה על גיטין

Link: [https://torahapp.org/share/book/Chokhmat%20Shlomo%20on%20Gittin](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Chokhmat%20Shlomo%20on%20Gittin)

16th-century glosses by the Maharshal focused on establishing the correct text of the Talmud.
