# Rashash on Taanit

Link: [https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashash%20on%20Taanit](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashash%20on%20Taanit)

19th-century annotations based on classes that the author, Rabbi Shmuel Strashun, delivered in a synagogue in Lithuania.
