# Gilyon HaShas on Keritot

גליון הש"ס על כריתות

Link: [https://torahapp.org/share/book/Gilyon%20HaShas%20on%20Keritot](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Gilyon%20HaShas%20on%20Keritot)
