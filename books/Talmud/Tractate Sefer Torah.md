# Tractate Sefer Torah

מסכת ספר תורה

Link: [https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah)

כתיבת ספר תורה וכיצד יש לנהוג בו; זהה כמעט לתחילת מסכת סופרים.

Writing and treatment of a Torah scroll; almost identical to the beginning of Tractate Soferim.

Sections: Chapter-Halakhah

Location: Talmud/Bavli/Minor Tractates

## Chapter 1

###### Tractate Sefer Torah 1:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/1:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/1:1)


אין כותבין ספר תורה לא על עורות בהמה טמאה ולא על עורות חיה טמאה ולא תופרין אותן בגידין ולא כורכין בשערן. הלכה למשה מסיני (אלא) שיהא כותבין על עורות בהמה טהורה ועל עורות חיה טהורה ותופרין בגידין טהורים וכורכין בשערות טהורים וכותבין בדיו שהרי יש לו סמך מן המקרא ואני כותב על הספר בדיו (ירמיהו ל״ו:י״ח):


It is not permitted to write a scroll of the Torah¹ on skins of ritually unclean cattle or on skins of ritually unclean wild animals, nor may they² be sewn with [their] sinews or [the parchment rolls of *tefillin*] be wound with their hair. It is an oral prescription delivered to Moses at Sinai that [these] shall be written on the skins of ritually clean cattle, or on skins of ritually clean wild animals, that they shall be sewn together with ritually clean sinews, [that the *tefillin* rolls] shall be wound with ritually clean hair, and that the writing be done with ink, because [this latter requirement] has support in Scripture, *And I wrote them with ink in the book*.³

footnotes:
¹ The scroll of the Pentateuch used for the lections in public worship.
² The parchment sheets of which the scroll is made up.
³ Jer. 36, 18.


###### Tractate Sefer Torah 1:2

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/1:2](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/1:2)


כותבין על עורות נבלות ועל עורות טרפות ואין חוששין שמא עורות לבובות הן:


It is permitted to write [sacred scrolls] on skins of *nebeloth* and on skins of *ṭerefoth* and there is no need to consider the possibility of their having been pierced at the animal’s heart.⁴

footnotes:
⁴ It was a heathen practice to remove the heart from a live animal to offer it as a sacrifice, and this, in Rabbinic law (Mishnah ‘A.Z. 29b, Sonc. ed., p. 145) rendered the entire animal, including its skin, forbidden as an idolatrous offering.


###### Tractate Sefer Torah 1:3

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/1:3](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/1:3)


לוקחין עורות מכל מקום לספרים ולתפילין ומזוזות ואין חוששין שמא עורות לבובין הן:


Skins⁵ for the [sacred] books, *tefillin* and *mezuzoth* may be bought anywhere, and one need not consider the possibility that they might be skins that were pierced at the animal’s heart.

footnotes:
⁵ Before they were dressed; but dressed skins may be bought only from trustworthy persons who can testify that the dressing had been done in accordance with the law.


###### Tractate Sefer Torah 1:4

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/1:4](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/1:4)


כותבין על הקלף במקום בשר ועל הגויל במקום שער ואם שינה בזה ובזה אל יקרא בו:


The writing on *ḳelaf* must be on the side of the flesh, and on *gewil* it must be on the side of the hair; and if one changed the one for the other [the scroll] may not be used for the lection [in the statutory services].


###### Tractate Sefer Torah 1:5

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/1:5](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/1:5)


אין כותבין לא על גבי דיפתרא ולא על גבי פינקס ולא על גבי נייר מחוק לא בשחור ולא בשיחור ולא בקומוס ולא בקנקנתום ולא נותנים קנקנתום בדיו:


[Sacred texts]⁶ may not be written on *difthera*, a tablet or erased paper;⁷ nor with coal,⁸ shoe-black, or ink containing gum or vitriol. Vitriol may not be added to the ink.

footnotes:
⁶ Mentioned in Rule 1.
⁷ Others ‘palimpsest’. GRA in Sof. I, 5 omits ‘erased’.
⁸ Or, ‘black pigment’.


###### Tractate Sefer Torah 1:6

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/1:6](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/1:6)


אמר רבי יעקב משום רבי מאיר נותנין קנקנתום בדיו אמר רבי יהודה היה רבי מאיר אומר הוא מן התורה בקנקנתום חוץ מפרשת סוטה אמר רבי יעקב לא היה אומר חוץ מפרשת סוטה אלא שבמקדש מפני שהיא נמחקת:


R. Jacob said in the name of R. Meir: Vitriol may be added to the ink. R. Judah said: R. Meir’s statement about [writing with ink containing] vitriol applies to [the whole] Torah⁹ except the section dealing with a suspected wife.¹⁰ R. Jacob retorted: [R. Meir] said, ‘Except the section dealing with a suspected wife’, [by which he meant that] only in the special scroll¹¹ which [was used] in the Temple [must the ink contain no vitriol] because [the writing] had to be blotted out.¹² 23f.

footnotes:
⁹ Cf. ‘Erub. 13a [Sonc. ed., p. 82).
¹⁰ Num. 5, 11ff.
¹¹ A small scroll on which were written the curses that would befall an unfaithful wife.
¹² Cf. Num. ibid.


###### Tractate Sefer Torah 1:7

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/1:7](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/1:7)


אמר רבי שמעון בן לקיש משום רבי מאיר משום ר׳ ישמעאל שנותנים קנקנתום בדיו:


R. Simeon b. Laḳish said in the name of R. Meir, who had it from R. Ishmael, that vitriol may be added to the ink.


###### Tractate Sefer Torah 1:8

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/1:8](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/1:8)


אין כותבין עברית ולא מדית ולא עילמת ולא יונית שבעים זקנים כתבו התורה לתלמי המלך כתיבה יונית והיה אותו היום קשה לישראל כיום שעשו את העגל שלא היתה תורה יכולה להתרגם כל צרכה:


[Sacred texts]¹³ may not be written in [obsolete] Hebrew characters or in the Median, Elamitic or Greek languages.¹⁴ and *mezuzoth*. Seventy Elders wrote the Torah for king Ptolemy¹⁵). in Greek, and that day was as ominous for Israel as the day on which the Golden Calf was made, since the Torah could not be adequately translated.

footnotes:
¹³ Mentioned in Rule 1.
¹⁴ According to Meg. 9a (Sonc. ed., p. 48) it is permitted to write the Torah in Greek, though this is not allowed in the case of tefillin
¹⁵ Assumed to be Ptolemy Philadelphus who reigned in Egypt, 285-247 B.C.E., and who arranged for the Greek Version, the Septuagint, to be made, as narrated in Sof. I, 8 (cf. the notes ad loc.


###### Tractate Sefer Torah 1:9

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/1:9](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/1:9)


י״ג דברים שינו בה אלהים ברא בראשית. אעשה אדם בצלם ודמות ויכל בששי וישבות בשביעי. זכר ונקבה בראו (בראשית ה׳:ב׳) הבה ארדה (שם יא) ותצחק שרה בקרוביה לאמר (שם יח). כי באפם הרגו שור וברצונם עקרו אבוס (שם מט) ויקח משה את אשתו ואת בניו וירכיבם על נושא בני אדם (שמות ד׳:כ׳). ומושב בני ישראל אשר ישבו בארץ כנען ובארץ מצרים שלשים שנה ות׳ שנה (שם יב). לא חמד אחד מהם נשאתי (במדבר ט״ז:ט״ו). ואת צעירת הרגלים (ויקרא י״א:כ״ב). אשר חלק ה׳ אלהיך אתם לכל העמים להאיר בהם תחת כל השמים (דברים ד׳:י״ט). ומעלין (רשות) לשמש או לירח או לכל צבא השמים אשר לא צויתי לעבדם (שם יז):


They [all] introduced [the same] thirteen alterations:¹⁶ ‘God created in the beginning’; ‘I shall make a man in image and likeness’; ‘And He finished on the sixth [day] and rested on the seventh [day]’; ‘Male and female He created him’; ‘Come let Me go down’; ‘And Sarah laughed among her relatives, saying’; ‘For in their anger they slew oxen and in their self-will they digged up a stall’; ‘And Moses took his wife and his children and set them upon a carrier of men’; ‘Now the time that the children of Israel dwelt in the land of Canaan and in the land of Egypt was four hundred and thirty years’; ‘I have not taken one desirable thing from them’; ‘And the [beast] with small legs’; ‘Which the Lord thy God hath allotted unto all the peoples to give light under the whole heaven’; ‘And they offer sacrifices to the sun or the moon or any of the host of heaven which I commanded should not be served’.

footnotes:
¹⁶ For a comparison of these variants with the Heb. text, cf. on Sof. I, 8.


###### Tractate Sefer Torah 1:10

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/1:10](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/1:10)


אין כותבין בזהב מעשה בתורתן של אלכסנדרים שהיו כל אזכרותיה כתובים בזהב ובא מעשה לפני חכמים ואמרו תיגנז:


[Sacred scrolls] must not be written in gold. It is related that, in an Alexandrian scroll¹⁷ of the Torah, the divine names were written in gold, and when the matter came before the Sages they decided that it should be stored away.¹⁸

footnotes:
¹⁷ Cf. note to Sof. I, 9.
¹⁸ lit. ‘it shall be hidden’, i.e. not used for the lections.


###### Tractate Sefer Torah 1:11

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/1:11](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/1:11)


עברית שכתבו תרגום תרגום שכתבו עברית האזינו שעשו רצוף או שעשו את המסורג שלא כהלכתו אל יקרא בו:


If Hebrew words were written in Aramaic or Aramaic words in Hebrew, if *Ha’azinu* was written in continuous lines,¹⁹ or if the breaking up [of the lines] was not done according to the rule, the scroll may not be used for the lections.

footnotes:
¹⁹ Cf. Sof. I, 10, n. 44, for the correct arrangement.


###### Tractate Sefer Torah 1:12

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/1:12](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/1:12)


גמר כל הספר ושייר בו פחות מדף מסרגו ועושה אותו דף קטן ואינו נמנע:


If one finished [writing nearly] all the scroll [of the Torah] and less than a column of text remained, one need not hesitate to extend the writing so as to make of this a small column on its own.²⁰

footnotes:
²⁰ The extension of a short passage over a full column is done either by enlarging the characters or, according to others, by making each successive line shorter than the one preceding it.


###### Tractate Sefer Torah 1:13

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/1:13](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/1:13)


פתוחה שעשאה סתומה וסתומה שעשאה פתוחה הרי זו תיגנז איזו היא פתוחה כל שהתחיל מראש השיטה כמה יהא בסוף השיטה ויתחיל מראש השיטה והיא נקראת פתוחה כדי לכתוב שם של שלש אותיות:


If an open section was written as a closed one or *vice versa*, the scroll must be stored away. What is an open section? One which commences at the beginning of a line. How much space must there be at the end of a line so that the section beginning at the next line may be called an open one? A space on which may be written a name of three letters.²¹

footnotes:
²¹ The opinion of Maimonides, which has been adopted, is that space must be left for three words, each of three letters (cf. Y.D. CCLXXV, §2).


###### Tractate Sefer Torah 1:14

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/1:14](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/1:14)


גמר כל הפרשה בסוף הדף אם שייר שיטה אחת ופרשה סתומה היא מתחיל באמצע השיטה ואם פתוחה היא מתחיל מלמעלה ומניח את השיטה ההיא:


Should an entire section be finished at the end of a column, if one [blank] line remained and [the next] section is a closed one, [the scribe] begins it in the middle of the line; but if it is an open section, he begins at the top [of the next column] and leaves that line [blank].²²

footnotes:
²² Cf. Sof. I, 14.



## Chapter 2

###### Tractate Sefer Torah 2:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/2:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/2:1)


מניחין בין שם לשם כדי שיהיו ניכרים ובאותיות כדי שלא יהיו מעורבין עירב את האותיות או שהפסיק באמצע השם אל יקרא בו:


Sufficient space must be left between words¹ so that they may be distinguished, and between letters so that they be not mixed. If the letters were mixed up or a break made in the middle of a word, the scroll must not be used for the lection in the statutory services.

footnotes:
¹ lit. ‘between name and name’.


###### Tractate Sefer Torah 2:2

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/2:2](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/2:2)


מביאין בין דפין שתי אותיות משם גדול אבל לא משם קטן ואם היה משם קטן בפני עצמו אסור:


[It is permitted] to insert between columns two letters from a long word, but not from a short one. If, however, [the part of a longer word forms] a short word on its own,² (he will reign) the last two letters can form an independent short word *lak* (to thee). it is forbidden [to insert it between the columns].

footnotes:
² e.g. in a word like yimlok


###### Tractate Sefer Torah 2:3

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/2:3](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/2:3)


מניחין תורה ריוח שתי אצבעות ובנביאים ובחומשין ריוח הגודל:


In a Torah scroll the space of two finger-breadths must be left [between columns], but in scrolls of the Prophets and in those of Pentateuchs³ a space of one thumb-breadth [only].

footnotes:
³ Intended for ordinary reading or study, and not for the public lections.


###### Tractate Sefer Torah 2:4

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/2:4](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/2:4)


מניחין (מלמעלן) [מלמטן] בתורה ריוח טפח ומלמעלה שני חלקים בטפח ובנביאים ובחומשין שלש אצבעות מלמטן ושתים מלמעלן כן וכן אם רצה להוסיף מוסיף ובלבד שלא יהיו זה וזה מרובין מן הכתב:


In the lower margin of a Torah scroll the space of a handbreadth is left, and in the upper margin two-thirds of a handbreadth;⁴ the upper margin must be three finger-breadths wide. but in scrolls of the Prophets and Pentateuchs three finger-breadths in the lower margin and two finger-breadths in the upper. In all these cases⁵ if it is desired to extend [the margins] this may be done, provided they do not exceed [the space of] the written matter.

footnotes:
⁴ lit. ‘two parts of a handbreadth’. This is also the reading of V and M in Sof. II, 4. According to Men. 30a (Sonc. ed., p. 195) and H in Sof. loc. cit.
⁵ lit. ‘here and there’.


###### Tractate Sefer Torah 2:5

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/2:5](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/2:5)


מניחין בין ספר לספר בתורה ריוח ארבעה שיטין ובנביא של תרי עשר ג' שיטין מניחין בסוף הספר ובתחלתו כדי להקים ובתורה לא נתנו שיעור אלא עושה עמוד בסוף הספר ואינו צריך לעשות כן אלא בתחילתן:


Between the books in a Torah scroll a vacant space of four lines must be left, and in a scroll containing the Twelve Prophets three lines.
At the end of a scroll and at its beginning sufficient [parchment] must be left to wind⁶. V reads *lehaḳim* ‘to raise up’. [it round the scroll]. In a Torah scroll no limit has been prescribed, but a roller should be attached at the end of the scroll and there is no need to do this at its beginning.⁷

footnotes:
⁶ Reading with many authorities lehaḳḳif
⁷ This regulation does not agree with Sof. II, 5.


###### Tractate Sefer Torah 2:6

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/2:6](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/2:6)


אין פוחתין ביריעה פחות מג׳ דפים ואין מוסיפין על שמונה ובקלפים לא נתנו שיעור אלא מוסיף כל מה שהוא רוצה ובלבד שלא יפחת מג' דפין:


A sheet of a Torah scroll must contain not less than three columns and not more than eight. In the case of *ḳelaf* parchments no limit was prescribed, [62*b*] and one may add [as many columns] as desired provided only there are not less than three columns [to the sheet].


###### Tractate Sefer Torah 2:7

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/2:7](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/2:7)


גמר כל הספר ושייר בו דף אחד עושה אותו יריעה קטנה בפני עצמה ואינו נמנע:


If one finished [writing nearly] all the book⁸ and one column of text remained, one need not hesitate to make of it a separate small sheet.

footnotes:
⁸ This refers either to one book of the Prophets or to several of them written in one scroll.


###### Tractate Sefer Torah 2:8

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/2:8](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/2:8)


יריעה שבלתה לא יטול שנים ויחזיר שנים אלא נוטל ג׳ ומחזיר ג׳ ומה שהוא מחזיר כמדת כתב הראשון:


If a sheet [of a Torah scroll] is worn out, one may not remove two [columns only] and replace them by two new columns; but three columns should be removed and replaced by three new ones, and the new columns must be written in the same size as the original script.


###### Tractate Sefer Torah 2:9

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/2:9](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/2:9)


שיעור הדף כדי שהוא רואהו ובקטן לא יפחות מטפח ר' יוסי בר יהודה אומר לא יפחות מג׳ אצבעות:


The width of a column [in a Torah scroll] must be such as to be recognizable at one glance,⁹ and in a small scroll not less than a handbreadth. R. Jose b. Judah says: It must not be less than the width of three fingers.

footnotes:
⁹ Accordingly, not more than three Hebrew words, each of ten letters, may be written in a line. If the column is wider, the reader experiences difficulty in passing from line to line.


###### Tractate Sefer Torah 2:10

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/2:10](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Sefer%20Torah/r/2:10)


לא יעשה ארכו יתר על רחבו ולא רחבו יתר על ארכו אבל ממציעו הוא ועושה אותו מן המובחר:


The height¹⁰ of [a scroll] must not be greater than its circumference,¹¹ nor its circumference greater than its height, but they must be exactly alike,¹² and this is the most correct procedure.

footnotes:
¹⁰ lit. ‘length’.
¹¹ When rolled up, so according to B.B. 14a (Sonc. ed., p. 67); lit. ‘its width’.
¹² lit. ‘takes the middle course’.


###### Tractate Sefer Torah 2:11

הכותב שני שמות של קודש מקיים את הראשון ומחטיב את האחרון רבי יהודה אומר אם היה האחרון בסוף הדף מחטיב ומוחק את הראשון ומקיים את האחרון:


If [a scribe] wrote a sacred name twice,¹ he retains the first and erases the latter.² R. Judah says: If the latter occurred at the end of a column, he erases the first and retains the latter.³

footnotes:
¹ lit. ‘he who writes two sacred names’.
² The first is retained because it was properly written; the second is erased because it was written in error.
³ [In Sof. V, 1, the reading is: ‘R. Judah says: If the first occurs at the end of a column’. Here H reads: ‘If the latter occurs at the beginning of a column’. Either of these must be substituted for V because the idea is that] it spoils the appearance to leave a blank space at the beginning of the top line of a column.

שני שמות של חול מקיים את הראשון ומוחק את האחרון רבי יהודה אומר אם היה האחרון בראש הדף מוחק את הראשון ומקיים את האחרון:


If [the scribe wrote] a secular noun twice,⁴ he retains the first and wipes away the latter.⁵ R. Judah says: If the latter was at the top of a column,⁶ he wipes away the first and retains the latter.

footnotes:
⁴ lit. ‘two secular names’.
⁵ While the ink is still wet.
⁶ [Cf. Sof. V, 2, n. 6.]

הכותב יהודה ולא כתב בו דלית יתלנו מלמעלה את השם כתב השם וכתב את יהודה עושה דלית הא ומוחק את ההא האחרונה (ויתלה את דלית):


If a scribe writes *Yehudah* and omits the *daleth* he inserts it above the line. If he intended to write the Tetragrammaton⁷. Lit. ‘the name, he wrote the name’, apparently a misprint. and wrote *Yehudah*⁸. instead, he alters the *daleth* to *he*⁹. and wipes away the final letter *he*.

footnotes:
⁷ The four-lettered name Yod-he-waw-he
⁸ Yod-he-waw-daleth-he
⁹ This is done by adding a short vertical line to the daleth

היה צריך לכתוב את יהודה ונתכוין וכתב את השם אע״פ שנתן בו דלית מחטיבו וכותב יהודה אחר:


If it was required to write *Yehudah* but [the scribe] intended to write the Tetragrammaton, although he [inadvertently] inserted the *daleth*,¹⁰ he erases it and writes another *Yehudah*.¹¹

footnotes:
¹⁰ Thus writing the noun correctly but without intention.
¹¹ Because the writing must be both intentional and correct.

היה צריך לכתוב את השם ונתכוין וכתב יהודה אע״פ שנתן בו דלית מוחק את הא האחרונה ויכתוב דלית הא ר׳ יהודה אומר יחזור עליו הקולמוס ויחדשנו אמרו לו אין זה מן המובחר:


If it was required to write the Tetragrammaton but [the scribe] intended to write *Yehudah*, although he inserted the *daleth*¹² was not only intended but also written correctly, in the wrong place. [Cf. the variant reading in Sof. V, 3.] he wipes away the final *he* and alters [the preceding] *daleth* into *he*.¹³ R. Judah says: He should [also] go over the entire name with the reed-pen and so sanctify it. The Sages said to him: This is not the best procedure.

footnotes:
¹² So that Yehudah
¹³ The letters being almost the same and the alteration so slight, the Tetragrammaton is deemed to have been written intentionally and correctly.

הכותב את השם וטעה אות אחת יתלנו מלמעלה טעה את השם כולו יתלנו בין השיטין כדברי ר׳ יהודה ר׳ יוסי אומר מוחק מה שלפניו וכותב את השם וחוזר ותולה מה שמוחק ר׳ חנניה בן עקשיא אומר צריך לגנוז את כל היריעה:


If in writing the Tetragrammaton he made an error [by omitting] one letter he should suspend it above [the line]. If one erred [by omitting] all the name¹⁴ he should suspend it between the lines. So R. Judah; R. Jose says: [The scribe] wipes away the letters preceding it, writes the Tetragrammaton [in their place] and suspends [above the line] the part which he had wiped away. R. Ḥananiah b. ‘Aḳashia says: All the sheet must be stored away.

footnotes:
¹⁴ The Tetragrammaton.

טעה וכתב יסגירני אל אל עויל (איוב ט״ז:י״א) מוחק את הראשון ומקיים את האחרון:


If one wrote *Alef-lamed* by mistake three¹⁵ times in the clause *God delivereth me to the ungodly*,¹⁶ one retains¹⁷ the first and erases¹⁸ the latter.¹⁹ which, having been intended in error to stand for the divine name, is treated in accordance with Rule 1 above. By quoting an illustration from Job, the inference to be drawn is that the regulation with respect to a Torah scroll holds good also of the Prophets and the Hagiographa.

¹⁷ This is the reading in Sof. V, 5. V has ‘wipes away’.
¹⁸ So GRA in Sof. V, 5. V has ‘retains’.
¹⁹ The second ’el

הכותב את השם אפילו המלך שואל בשלומו לא ישיבנו אלא היה כותב שנים שלשה שמות הרי זה מפסיק ביניהם ומשיב:


When one is writing the Tetragrammaton, one may not respond even if the king greets him;²⁰ but if one was about to write two or three divine names successively²¹ (Ex. 34, 6) or Deut. 6, 4. one may pause between them and respond.

²⁰ All one’s attention must be concentrated on the writing of the divine name.

המוחק אות אחת מן השם עובר בלא תעשה אמר ר׳ ישמעאל לכך נאמר ואבדתם את שמם מן המקום ההוא ונאמר לא תעשון כן לה׳ אלהיכם (דברים י״ב:ג׳-ד׳) אבל דיו שנטפה על גבי הכתב מותר למחקו שלא היה כונתו אלא לתקן:


One who wipes away²⁶ a single letter of a divine name transgresses a negative commandment. R. Ishmael²⁷ says: On account of this it is stated, *Ye shall destroy their name out of that place;*²⁸ and it is also stated, *Ye shall not do so unto the Lord your God*.²⁹ 4; i.e. the name of God must never be erased. It is permissible, however, to wipe away ink that dropped on the writing,³⁰ since one’s intention is only to rectify it.

footnotes:
²⁶ In Sof. V, 6, ‘erases’.
²⁷ ‘R. Simeon’ in Sof. loc. cit.
²⁸ Deut. 12, 3.
²⁹ ibid.
³⁰ Even of a divine name.

אין עושין מגילה והלל לתינוקת ר׳ יהודה מתיר מן בראשית עד המבול מן ויקרא עד ויהי ביום השמיני ושאר כל הספרים אסור ואם עתיד להוסיף עליהם מותר:


It is not permissible to make a scroll [of the *Shema‘*]³¹ or of the *Hallel* for children.³² R. Judah permits [the writing of a scroll] from *In the beginning*³³ to the story of the Flood,³⁴ VI, 8. and from the beginning of Leviticus to *And it came to pass on the eighth day;*³⁵ but [in the case of] all the other books [of Scripture the writing of extracts is] forbidden.³⁶ If, however, one intends to add to them,³⁷ it is permissible [to write extracts from the Scriptures in scrolls].

footnotes:
³¹ So Sof. V, 9; lacking in V here.
³² Extracts from the Scriptures may not be written on scrolls intended for children’s instruction.
³³ Gen. 1, 1.
³⁴ ibid.
³⁵ Lev. 9, 1.
³⁶ Only in the case of the Pentateuch were the two extracts mentioned allowed to be written for the instruction of children.
³⁷ To complete all the book.

הכותב שם על בשרו לא ירחץ ולא יסוך ר׳ יוסי אומר מותר ובלבד שלא ישפשף על קרן הפרה ועל כרעי המטה גוררן וגונזן על האבן שומטה וגונזה:


If a person writes a divine name on his body, he must neither bathe nor anoint himself.⁵⁰ R. Jose says: This is permitted provided only that he does not rub it off. [If he wrote it] on the horn of a cow or on the legs of a bed,⁵¹ he scrapes it off⁵², ‘he chops it off’ (the horn or the leg) for *goreron*, ‘he scrapes them off’ and stores it away.⁵³ [If he wrote it] on a stone, he detaches it and stores it away.

footnotes:
⁵⁰ So that the name may not be washed off.
⁵¹ For magical purposes.
⁵² [Perhaps the reading should be godedo
⁵³ Out of respect for the divine name. The text has ‘scrapes them … stores them’.

המחתך בספרים בחדשים מותר בישנים אסור (במקום הספרים) במקק הספרים ובמקק התפילין ובמקק מטפחות מהספרים הרי אלו יגנזו אבל לא יניחם אלא במקום תורפה והם מרקבין מאליהם:


Should one cut off a strip of a scroll,⁵⁴ it may [be used for ordinary purposes] if it is a new one,⁵⁵ but if it is an old scroll⁵⁶ [the use of the strip] is forbidden. As regards worn-out scrolls, phylacteries and wraps of scrolls, these must be stored away.⁵⁷ They should not be deposited [in an unclean place]⁵⁸ but in a deserted spot where they decay naturally.⁵⁹

footnotes:
⁵⁴ lit. ‘in scrolls’.
⁵⁵ Being new, the scroll had not yet been consecrated.
⁵⁶ Which has been consecrated for holy use.
⁵⁷ Having been once consecrated they may no longer be used for ordinary purposes.
⁵⁸ The words in brackets are added in H.
⁵⁹ lit. ‘of their own accord’.
