# Chokhmat Shlomo on Sukkah

חכמת שלמה על סוכה

Link: [https://torahapp.org/share/book/Chokhmat%20Shlomo%20on%20Sukkah](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Chokhmat%20Shlomo%20on%20Sukkah)

16th-century glosses by the Maharshal focused on establishing the correct text of the Talmud.
