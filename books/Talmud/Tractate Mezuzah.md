# Tractate Mezuzah

מסכת מזוזה

## Chapter 1

###### Tractate Mezuzah 1:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/1:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/1:1)


אין כותבין מזוזות לא על גבי עורות בהמה טמאה ולא על גבי עורות חיה טמאה אבל כותבין על עורות נבלות ועל עורות טרפות ואין חוששין שמא עורות לבובין הם


It is not permitted to write *mezuzoth* on the skins of ritually unclean cattle or on skins of ritually unclean wild animals. It is permitted, however, to write them on skins of *nebeloth* and *ṭerefoth*, and there is no need to consider the possibility of their having been pierced at the animal’s heart.¹

footnotes:
¹ There was a heathen practice to remove the heart from an animal while it was still alive and offer it as a sacrifice. Rabbinic law regards such an animal as an idolatrous offering (cf. Mishnah ‘A.Z. 29b, Sonc. ed., p. 145) and forbids its entire use, including the skin.


###### Tractate Mezuzah 1:2

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/1:2](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/1:2)


צריך להניח שיטה אחת לכל צד כדברי רשב״א


It is required to leave [a space of] one line on every side [of the text of the *mezuzah*], according to R. Simeon b. Eleazar.


###### Tractate Mezuzah 1:3

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/1:3](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/1:3)


נקבה במקום [הכתב פסולה במקום] הריוח כשרה עשא הווין זיינין זיינין ווין כפופים פשוטים פשוטים כפופים פתוחים סתומים סתומים פתותים או ששנה אותיותיה או שנכתבה עברית הרי זו פסולה


If a hole was made in the place of the script it is unfit for religious use; but if it occurred in the margin it remains fit.
If the letter *waw* [in a *mezuzah*] was shaped like a *zayin* or *vice versa*, curved letters like straight-lined letters² like the final *kaf.* or *vice versa*, open letters closed³ like the final *mem.* or *vice versa*, or if the shape of its letters had been altered [in any other way], or if it was written in [obsolete] Hebrew, it is unfit for religious use.

footnotes:
² e.g. an ordinary kaf
³ An ordinary mem


###### Tractate Mezuzah 1:4

בית התבן בית העצים בית האוצר הרי אלו פטורים רפת הבקר פטור אם היו הנשים נאותות בו חייב הצריפים והבורגנין והלקטיות והבורסקיות הרי אלו פטורים מן המזוזה

Cone-shaped huts,⁶, I, p. 6.] travellers’ stations, field sheds⁷, p. 226, and *Talm. Arch.* I, p. 276, n. 81, reads *hilaḳṭiyoth*. Maimonides understood the term to mean ‘summer-house’, thatched on top and open on the four sides. More probably it was used for stacking hay, etc., where it was dried by the wind.] and tanneries are exempt from *mezuzah*.⁸

⁵ In which to adorn themselves; but if they use any of the structures for bathing it is exempt (cf. Yoma 11a, Sonc. ed., p. 48; Y.D. CCLXXXVI, §2).
⁶ [Used by watchers in a field or by hunters; Krauss, Talmudische Archäologie

###### Tractate Mezuzah 1:5

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/1:5](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/1:5)


בית המרחץ פטורה אם יש לה בית דירה פתח החיצון חייב


A bath-house is exempt, but if a dwelling-house adjoins it the outer doorway is liable.


###### Tractate Mezuzah 1:6

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/1:6](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/1:6)


פתח שבזוית אין בו אלא מזוזה אחת פתח שהוא עשוי בנסרים אין בו אלא מזוזה אחת חייב ר׳ אליעזר בן יעקב פוטר


A corner doorway that has only one doorpost, [or] a doorway made in [a structure of] boards which has only one doorpost is liable. R. Eliezer b. Jacob declares it exempt.


###### Tractate Mezuzah 1:7

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/1:7](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/1:7)


הכיפין פטורות ר׳ מאיר ור׳ יהודה מחייבין אבל הכל שוין שאם היה הבנין מלמטה והכיפין מלמעלה הרי אלו חייבות במזוזה


Arched doorways are exempt, but R. Meir and R. Judah declare that they are liable. All, however, agree that if they have the [usual] structure below⁹ and the arched section above they are liable to *mezuzah*.

footnotes:
⁹ Being four handbreadths wide and reaching to a height of ten handbreadths (cf. ‘Erub, 11b, Sonc. ed., p. 69).


###### Tractate Mezuzah 1:8

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/1:8](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/1:8)


שערי העיר שערי הרחובות שערי העזרות הרי אלו פטורים ושל הלשכות שהיו במקדש היו פטורות מן המזוזה והלשכה של בית הפרוה שהיתה בית דירה לכהן גדול ז׳ ימים בשנה וסוכות היוצרים הרי אלו חייבות במזוזה ור׳ יוסי פוטר:


City gates, the gates of open places¹⁰ and the gates of Temple Courts are exempt.¹¹.] [The doorways of] chambers in the Temple were exempt from *mezuzah*, but the Chamber of the Counsellors,¹², one of the Temple chambers, in agreement with Yoma 10a (Sonc. ed., p. 45). V and H read: *lishkath shel beth happarwah*, ‘the chamber of the Parwah house’, Parwah being the name of its Persian builder (cf. *ibid.* 35a, Sonc. ed., p. 161, n. 6). which served as a residence for the High Priest on seven days in the year,¹³ 2a, Sonc. ed., p. 1). and potters’ booths are subject to *mezuzah*. R. Jose declares them exempt.

¹⁰ Where people assemble for special prayers or to discuss public matters.
¹¹ [But according to Yoma 11a, Sonc. ed., p. 47, city gates require a mezuzah
¹² Reading lishkath parhedrin
¹³ Before the Day of Atonement (cf. ibid.



## Chapter 2

###### Tractate Mezuzah 2:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/2:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/2:1)


המזוזה נכתבת על הימין בכניסתו מן השוק לבית שער וביציאתו מן החצר לבית שער


The *mezuzah* is placed¹ on the right-hand side of the entrance from the street into the lodge, and [on the right-hand side of] the exit from the courtyard into the lodge.²

footnotes:
¹ lit. ‘written’.
² It refers to where the lodge is in front of a courtyard and has two doorways, one facing the street and the other facing the courtyard.


###### Tractate Mezuzah 2:2

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/2:2](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/2:2)


גננא שיש לה פצים מן החצר נותן דרך יציאתו מן הגנה לחצר ואם יש לה בית שער נותן מכאן ומכאן


If a garden has a doorpost where it adjoins the courtyard, the *mezuzah* is placed [on the right-hand side of] the exit from the garden into the courtyard; and if a lodge is attached to it,³ *mezuzoth* are to be placed on both doorways [of the lodge].

footnotes:
³ Between the garden and the courtyard, with doorways leading to each of them.


###### Tractate Mezuzah 2:3

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/2:3](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/2:3)


קיטוניות שהן פתוחים לטרקלן נותן דרך הנעלה ואם היה רגיל על כולם נותן דרך כולם


Where small rooms open into a reception room,⁴ the *mezuzah* is placed on that doorway which is customarily used.⁵ If they are all customarily used, *mezuzoth* are placed on all the doorways.

footnotes:
⁴ Access to which is gained only through the small ones.
⁵ So according to H. V’s reading is corrupt.


###### Tractate Mezuzah 2:4

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/2:4](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/2:4)


בתים לפנים זה מזה נותן דרך כניסתו לכולם רבי יהודה אומר אם היה הסולם מטלטל לארבע רוחותיו


If a number of rooms have doorways leading from one room to the other,⁶ *mezuzoth* are placed at the entrance of each one of them. R. Judah says: If the ladder is movable on the four sides….⁷ is placed at every side’; but this also does not convey much sense.

footnotes:
⁶ lit. ‘rooms within one another’.
⁷ The sentence is obviously incomplete. H reads: ‘If the ladder is movable a mezuzah


###### Tractate Mezuzah 2:5

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/2:5](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/2:5)


פתח שהוא פתוח מן הגג נותן דרך כניסתו מן הגג אמר ר׳ נתן בר׳ יוסי בזה ב״ש מחייבין וב״ה פוטרין


If a doorway leads from a roof [into a room], the *mezuzah* is placed [on the right-hand side of] the entrance from the roof. R. Nathan b. R. Jose said: In such a case Beth Shammai hold that the doorway is subject to *mezuzah* and Beth Hillel hold that it is exempt.


###### Tractate Mezuzah 2:6

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/2:6](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/2:6)


סוכה שהיא לפנים מן הגג נותן דרך יציאתו מן הסוכה לגג ר׳ יוסי אומר מן הגג לסוכה


In a booth that is set back⁸ from [the edge of] a roof the *mezuzah* is to be placed [on the right-hand side of] the exit from the booth to the roof. R. Jose says: [On the right-hand side of the entrance] from the roof to the booth.

footnotes:
⁸ lit. ‘within’.


###### Tractate Mezuzah 2:7

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/2:7](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/2:7)


אכסדרה שהיא לפנים מן הבתים ואין לה יציאה ממקום אחר נותן דרך יציאתו מן האכסדרה לבתים ר׳ יוסי אומר דרך כניסתו מבתים לאכסדרה


In the case of a portico that is set back from the rooms and has no other exit,⁹ the *mezuzah* is placed [on the right-hand side of] the exit from the portico into the rooms. R. Jose says: [On the right-hand side of] the entrance from the rooms to the portico.

footnotes:
⁹ Except through one of the rooms.


###### Tractate Mezuzah 2:8

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/2:8](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/2:8)


פתח שהיא משמשת שני פתחים וכותל רחב טפח נותן מזוזה אחת כדברי ר׳ יהודה יותר מכאן שני מזוזות


If an entrance serves as two doorways and a wall [of not more than] one handbreath in width [separates them], only one *mezuzah* is to be affixed, according to R. Judah. [If the wall was] wider than that two *mezuzoth* are required.


###### Tractate Mezuzah 2:9

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/2:9](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/2:9)


המזוזה ניתנה מן החצי ולמעלה דברי ר״מ ר׳ יהודה אומר מטפח ולמטה ומטפח ולמעלה


The *mezuzah* is placed in the upper half of the doorpost;¹⁰ so R. Meir. R. Judah says: [It should be placed] below the handbreadth [from the top of the doorpost] and above one handbreadth [from the bottom].

footnotes:
¹⁰ lit. ‘from the half and above’. Another reading is: ‘on the upper third’; cf. the ruling in the name of Samuel in Men. 33a (Sonc. ed., p. 208).


###### Tractate Mezuzah 2:10

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/2:10](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/2:10)


חקק בכותל והרחיק טפח ונתנה הרי זו מזוזה כשרה יותר מכאן פסולה חקק במקל ונתנה הרי זו פסולה נתנה בשפופרת תלאה אחר הדלת אחר המזוזה ר׳ מאיר מכשיר ור׳ יהודה פוסל


If one made a groove in the wall at a distance of [not more than] one handbreadth [from the doorpost], and placed [the *mezuzah*] in it, the *mezuzah* is valid. [If the distance] was greater than that it is invalid.
If one made a groove in a stick and put the *mezuzah* in it, it is invalid. If one inserted [the *mezuzah*] in a tube and hung it up behind the door [or] behind the doorpost,¹¹ R. Meir declared it valid, but R. Judah declared it invalid.

footnotes:
¹¹ Hanging the stick on the doorpost.


###### Tractate Mezuzah 2:11

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/2:11](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/2:11)


בית חדש כיון שגמרו חייב במזוזה


A new house is subject to the obligation of *mezuzah* as soon as it is completed.


###### Tractate Mezuzah 2:12

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/2:12](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/2:12)


הדר בפונדק בארץ ישראל שלשים יום חייב במזוזה השוכר נותן מיד ובחוצה לארץ הרי זה פטור והשוכר נותן לאחר שלשים יום


One who lodges in an inn in the Land of Israel for thirty days is subject to the obligation of *mezuzah*, and if one rents [a house] he must affix [a *mezuzah*] forthwith. Outside the Land of Israel, [one who lives in an inn] is exempt, and one who rents [a house] must affix [a *mezuzah*] after thirty days.


###### Tractate Mezuzah 2:13

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/2:13](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/2:13)


השוכר בית מן הנכרי ומן הכותי נותן מזוזה מיד וכשיצא יטלנה והשוכר מישראל יתן מזוזה מיד ואם נתן לא יטול


[A Jew] who rents a house from a Gentile or from a Samaritan must affix a *mezuzah* forthwith, and when he quits he must remove it; but if one rents a house from an Israelite, he affixes the *mezuzah* forthwith and when he quits he must not remove it.


###### Tractate Mezuzah 2:14

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/2:14](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/2:14)


הלוקח בית מן הנכרי ומן הכותי חייב במזוזה ומישראל הרי זו בחזקתו


One who buys a house from a Gentile or a Samaritan is under the obligation of putting a *mezuzah*, but [if he bought it] from an Israelite it is presumed [to have been provided with the necessary *mezuzoth*].


###### Tractate Mezuzah 2:15

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/2:15](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Tractate%20Mezuzah/r/2:15)


הלוקח מלבן מן הנכרי לא ידליק עד שיבדקנו שהמזוזה נבדקת אחת לכ״ה שנה דברי ר״ש ר׳ יעקב אומר עד זמן מרובה אבל הכל שוין שאם היה מעביר ממקום למקום שצריכה בדיקה כיוצא בה מוכנות מים שברשות הרבים נבדקת אחת בשבוע ושברשות היחיד אחת לכ״ה שנה דברי ר׳ מאיר וחכמים אומרים אחד זה ואחד זה זה הכלל כל מקום שהוא בת ראש נבדק אחת בשבוע ושאינו בת ראש נבדק אחת לכ״ה שנה:


If one buys a brick-mould from a Gentile, he should not heat it before he had examined it.
[The scroll in] the *mezuzah* should be examined once in twenty-five years, according to R. Simeon. R. Jacob says: A much longer time [may be allowed to intervene]. Both, however, agree that if it was transferred from one place to another it requires examination.
In like manner, water-tanks¹², ‘stores’. in a public domain must be examined once in seven years, but those situated in a private domain once in twenty-five years, according to R. Meir; but the Sages say: In either case the general rule is that wherever there is a top cover¹³ instead of V’s *bath*. the examination takes place once in seven years, and where there is no top cover the examination takes place once in twenty-five years.

footnotes:
¹² lit. ‘machines’. H has mogeroth
¹³ Reading with H beth
