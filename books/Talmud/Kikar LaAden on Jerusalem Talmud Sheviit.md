# Kikar LaAden on Jerusalem Talmud Sheviit

Link: [https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kikar%20LaAden%20on%20Jerusalem%20Talmud%20Sheviit](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kikar%20LaAden%20on%20Jerusalem%20Talmud%20Sheviit)

Glosses on the Jerusalem originally published in the 18th century by Rabbi Chaim Yosef David Azulai, and attributed by him to a “holy person.”
