## Chapter 1


### Verse 2

###### Rashi on Zephaniah 1:2:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Zephaniah/r/1:2:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Zephaniah/r/1:2:1)


**אָסֹף אָסֵף.** לְשׁוֹן ׳כִּלָּיוֹן׳, כְּמוֹ (שופטים יח:כה) ״וְאָסַפְתָּ אֶת נַפְשֶׁךָ״ דְּמִיכָה. וְאָלֶ״ף חֲסֵרָה בְּתֵיבָה שְׁנִיָּה, שֶׁהֲרֵי הָיָה לוֹ לִכְתֹּב ״אֶאֱסֹף״, אֲבָל יֵשׁ כָּאֵלֶּה הַרְבֵּה, כְּמוֹ ״לֹא יָהֵל שָׁם עֲרָבִי״ (ישעיהו יג:כ), שֶׁהוּא כְּמוֹ ״לֹא יֶאֱהַל״.


**I will totally destroy** An expression of destruction, as in (Jud. 18:25) “and you will lose your life,” mentioned regarding Micah. The “aleph” is defective in the second word, for it should have been written אֶאֱסֹף There are many [defective alephs] like these, as in (Isa. 13:20) “no Arab shall pitch his tent there,” which is like יֶאֱהַל.

###### Rashi on Zephaniah 1:3:1

**and the stumbling blocks with the wicked** They are the pagan deities.

**the remnant of Baal** that there shall be no remnant of its remembrance.

### Verse 5

###### Rashi on Zephaniah 1:5:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Zephaniah/r/1:5:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Zephaniah/r/1:5:1)


**וְאֶת הַנִּשְׁבָּעִים לַה׳.** וְחוֹזְרִים וְנִשְׁבָּעִים בְּמַלְכָּם, בַּעֲבוֹדָה זָרָה שֶׁלָּהֶם. עוֹשִׂין עֲבוֹדָה זָרָה עִקָּר, לוֹמַר: אִם אֵין אַתָּה מַאֲמִין בִּשְׁבוּעַת ה׳, הֲרֵינִי נִשְׁבָּע לְךָ בַּעֲבוֹדָה זָרָה.


**who swear by the Lord** and go back and swear by their king, their pagan deity. They make the pagan deity the main one, saying, “If you do not believe the oath by God, I swear to you by the pagan deity.”

### Verse 9

###### Rashi on Zephaniah 1:9:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Zephaniah/r/1:9:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Zephaniah/r/1:9:1)


**הַדּוֹלֵג עַל הַמִּפְתָּן.** תִּרְגֵּם יוֹנָתָן: ״דְּמְהַלְכִין בְּנִימוּסֵי פְלִשְׁתָּאֵי״, שֶׁלֹּא הָיוּ דּוֹרְכִים עַל מִפְתַּן דָּגוֹן, שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר: ״עַל כֵּן לֹא יִדְרְכוּ כֹהֲנֵי דָגוֹן וְגוֹ׳״ (שמואל א ה:ה).


**who leaps over the threshold** *Jonathan* renders: who follows the customs of the Philistines, who would not step on the threshold of Dagon, as it is stated (I Sam. 5:5): “Therefore, the priests of Dagon... do not tread, etc.”

### Verse 11

###### Rashi on Zephaniah 1:11:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Zephaniah/r/1:11:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Zephaniah/r/1:11:1)


**יֹשְׁבֵי הַמַּכְתֵּשׁ.** ״נַחֲלָא דְקִדְרוֹן״, שֶׁעֲמֻקָּה כְּמַכְתֶּשֶׁת זֶה, תִּרְגֵּם יוֹנָתָן. וּמִדְרַשׁ אַגָּדָה: **מִשַּׁעַר הַדָּגִים** – זוֹ עַכּוֹ הַנְּתוּנָה בְּחֵיקוֹ שֶׁל הַיָּם, שֶׁשָּׁם נְצוֹדִים דָּגִים הַרְבֵּה.


**you inhabitants of Maktesh** The Kidron Valley, which is as deep as a mortar. This is according to *Targum Jonathan*. **from the fish gate** This is Acre, situated in the bosom of the sea, where many fish are caught.

**from the Mishneh** This is Lod, which is second to Jerusalem.


###### Rashi on Zephaniah 1:11:3

**from the hills** This is Sepphoris, which is situated at the tops of the mountains like a bird.


###### Rashi on Zephaniah 1:11:4

**inhabitants of Maktesh** This is Tiberias, which is deeper than all of [the rest of] the land of Israel.


###### Rashi on Zephaniah 1:11:5

**for the entire people of Canaan is cut off** *Jonathan* renders: For the entire nation, whose deeds resemble the deeds of the people of the land of Canaan, has been broken.


###### Rashi on Zephaniah 1:11:6

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Zephaniah/r/1:11:6](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Zephaniah/r/1:11:6)


**כָּל נְטִילֵי כֶסֶף.** ״כָּל עֲתִּירֵי נִכְסַיָּא״ טְעוּנֵי מַשָּׂא כֶּסֶף וְזָהָב, כְּמוֹ (משלי כז:ג) ״כֹּבֶד הָאֶבֶן וְנֵטֶל הַחוֹל״, לְשׁוֹן ׳מַשָּׂא׳.


**all those laden with silver** *Jonathan* renders: All those wealthy with property, laden with a burden of silver and gold; as in (Prov. 27:3) “The weight of stone, the burden of sand” an expression of a burden.



### Verse 12

###### Rashi on Zephaniah 1:12:1

**I will search Jerusalem with candles** I will judge their iniquities strictly.


###### Rashi on Zephaniah 1:12:2

**who are settled** float on their lees. [As in] (II Kings 6:6) “And the axe blade floated,” rendered in Aramaic: וּקְפָא פַרְזְלָה.

**who say in their heart, “The Lord shall do neither good...”** to those who obey His will.


###### Rashi on Zephaniah 1:12:4

**וְלֹא יָרֵעַ.** לְעוֹבְרֵי רְצוֹנוֹ.


**nor harm** to those who transgress His will.

## Chapter 2


### Verse 1

###### Rashi on Zephaniah 2:1:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Zephaniah/r/2:1:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Zephaniah/r/2:1:1)


**הִתְקוֹשְׁשׁוּ.** הִתְלַקְּטוּ וְאִסְפוּ יַחַד, כְּמוֹ ״לְקֹשֵׁשׁ קַשׁ״ (שמות ה:יב).


**Gather yourselves together** Gather and assemble together as in (Exod. 5:12) “To Gather a gathering.”


###### Rashi on Zephaniah 2:1:2

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Zephaniah/r/2:1:2](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Zephaniah/r/2:1:2)


**וָקוֹשּׁוּ.** מַעֲשֵׂיכֶם. הַשְׁווּ מַעֲשֵׂיכֶם לְדַעַת קוֹנְכֶם. וְרַבּוֹתֵינוּ זִכְרוֹנָם לִבְרָכָה דָּרְשׁוּ: קַשֵּׁט עַצְמְךָ וְאַחַר כָּךְ קַשֵּׁט אֲחֵרִים. עָשׂוּ שְׁנֵיהֶם לְשׁוֹן ׳הֶקֵּשׁ׳, אָדָם הַמַּשְׁוֶה עַצְמוֹ וּמְיַשֵּׁר דַּרְכּוֹ.


**gather together** your deeds. Conform your deeds to the will of Your Maker.


###### Rashi on Zephaniah 2:1:3

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Zephaniah/r/2:1:3](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Zephaniah/r/2:1:3)


**הַגּוֹי לֹא נִכְסָף.** תִּרְגֵּם יוֹנָתָן: ״דְּלָא חֲמַד לְמֵיתַב לְאוֹרָיְתָא״.


**O nation that has no desire** That has no desire to return to the Torah.

**כְּמֹץ עָבַר.** הֲרֵי זֶה מִקְרָא קָצָר, כְּמֹץ הָעוֹבֵר מִלִּפְנֵי רוּחַ, וּכְעָשָׁן הָעוֹבֵר מִלִּפְנֵי הַשֶּׁמֶשׁ. וְזֶהוּ תַּרְגּוּמוֹ שֶׁל ״עָבַר יוֹם״. כָּךְ תִּרְגֵּם יוֹנָתָן: ״תֶּהֱווֹן דָּמַן לְכְמוֹצָא דְּנָשֵׁב בֵּיהּ רוּחָא, וּכִתְנָנָא דְּעָדֵי מִן קֳדָם יוֹמָא״. נֻסָּח אַחֵר: כְּטוּלָא דְּעָדוֹ וְכוּ׳״, זֶהוּ לְשׁוֹן ׳צֵל׳.


**as chaff that passes... the sun** This is an ellipsis for, “As chaff that passes from before the wind, and as smoke that passes from before the sun.” This is the translation of עָבַר יוֹם, that passes the sun. In this manner, *Jonathan* renders: And you shall be like the chaff that the wind blows, like smoke that passes from before the sun. Another version: and like the shadow that passes, etc.; this is an expression of a shadow.

**הוֹי מֹרְאָה וְנִגְאָלָה.** עַד עַכְשָׁיו דִּבֵּר בְּנִינְוֵה, וְעַכְשָׁיו חוֹזֵר עַל יְרוּשָׁלַיִם: הוֹי, שֶׁתְּהֵא לְבַז וּלְסְחִי כְּרֹאִי, וּתְהֵא מְגֹאֶלֶת בִּצְחַן עֲוֹנָהּ. **מֹרְאָה** – כְּמוֹ ״מֻרְאָתוֹ״ (ויקרא א:טז), ״וְשַׂמְתִּיךְ כְּרֹאִי״ (נחום ג:ו).


**Woe to her who is filthy and polluted** Until now, Zephaniah was speaking of Ninveh, but now he refers to Jerusalem: Woe to her who is destined to be plundered and sullied like dung. It will be polluted with the filth of its iniquity. **filthy** as in (Lev. 1:16) “its crop”; and (Nahum 3:6) “And I will make you like dung.”

### Verse 19

###### Rashi on Zephaniah 3:19:1
