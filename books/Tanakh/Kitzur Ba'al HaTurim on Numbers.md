### Verse 2

###### Kitzur Ba'al HaTurim on Numbers 3:2:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kitzur%20Ba%27al%20HaTurim%20on%20Numbers/r/3:2:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kitzur%20Ba%27al%20HaTurim%20on%20Numbers/r/3:2:1)


**אהרן הבכר נדב** יש פסיק בין הבכור לנדב לומר שאינו חוזר על נדב כי נדב מת בלא בנים ואין נפקותא בבכורה שלו אלא חוזר על אהרן שהוא הבכור למשה:


**These are the names of the sons of Aharon, the firstborn, Nadav, and Avihu, Elazar and Isamar**. There is a pause between the words “the firstborn” and “Nadav” because it does not refer to Nadav who died without children, and there is no practical ramification of his being the firstborn. Rather, it refers to Aharon who was the firstborn ahead of Moshe.

..."Thus shall you bless" - the word thus (Heb. koh) comes to remind us of the merit of the verse "the boy and I will walk thus far" (Gen. 22) and also "thus shall be your offspring" and also "thus The LORD has blessed me." This is why the Priestly Blessing starts and finishes with the word peace and that is the meaning of "The LORD will bless His people with peace."
