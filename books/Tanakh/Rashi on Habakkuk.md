## Chapter 1


### Verse 1

###### Rashi on Habakkuk 1:1:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/1:1:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/1:1:1)


**הַמַּשָּׂא אֲשֶׁר חָזָה.** ״מַטַּל נְבוּאֲתָא״, מַה שֶּׁנָּשָׂא וְקִבֵּל בְּרוּחַ הַקֹּדֶשׁ.


**The prophecy** Heb. הַמַשָּׂא. The burden of prophecy - what he bore and received by the Holy Spirit. [after *Jonathan*]



### Verse 2

###### Rashi on Habakkuk 1:2:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/1:2:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/1:2:1)


**עַד אָנָה ה׳.** צוֹפֶה הָיָה בְּרוּח הַקֹּדֶשׁ שֶׁעָתִיד נְבוּכַדְנֶצַּר לִמְלֹךְ בַּכִּפָּה וְלִהְיוֹת מֵצֵר לְיִשְׂרָאֵל, כָּעִנְיָן שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר בִּנְבוּאָתוֹ ״כִּי הִנְנִי מֵקִים אֶת הַכַּשְׂדִּים וְגוֹ׳״, וְעַל זֹאת הָיָה מִתְאוֹנֵן וּמִתְפַּלֵּל.


**O Lord! How long** -He foresaw with the Holy Spirit that Nebuchadnezzar was destined to be the ruler of the world and to cause trouble for Israel, as the matter is stated in his prophecy (1:6): “For behold, I raise up the Chaldeans, etc.” He was complaining for this.


###### Rashi on Habakkuk 1:2:2

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/1:2:2](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/1:2:2)


**אֶזְעַק אֵלֶיךָ חָמָס.** אַזְכִּיר לְפָנֶיךָ חָמָס הֶעָשׂוּי לִי וְאֵינְךָ מוֹשִׁיעַ.


**I cry out to You of violence** I mention before You the violence committed against me, and You do not save me.



### Verse 3

###### Rashi on Habakkuk 1:3:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/1:3:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/1:3:1)


**אָוֶן.** בִּזָּה וְחָמָס.


**iniquity** plunder and violence.


###### Rashi on Habakkuk 1:3:2

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/1:3:2](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/1:3:2)

**and look upon mischief** You look upon this mischief, but You do not help.


###### Rashi on Habakkuk 1:3:3

**and the one who bears quarrel and strife endures** And this wicked man who bears quarrel and strife, will live, remain in existence, and prosper.

**וַיְהִי.** וְיִתְקַיֵּם אוֹתוֹ אֲשֶׁר רִיב וּמָדוֹן יִשָּׂא, כֵּן תַּרְגּוּם יוֹנָתָן.


**endures** He who bears quarrel and strife will endure. So did *Jonathan* render.



### Verse 4

###### Rashi on Habakkuk 1:4:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/1:4:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/1:4:1)


**עַל כֵּן תָּפוּג תּוֹרָה.** עַל זֶה שֶׁיִּשְׂרָאֵל רוֹאִין הַצְלָחָתוֹ שֶׁלּוֹ, תָּפִיג וְתַעֲבֹר תּוֹרָה מֵהֶם, וְיִשְׁמְעוּ לוֹ וְיִשְׁתַּחֲווּ לַצּוּרָה בְּבִקְעַת דּוּרָא.


**Therefore Torah is slackened** Because of this, that Israel sees his [the wicked man’s] success, the Torah will slacken and leave them; and they [Israel] will obey him [the wicked man] and prostrate themselves before the idol in the valley of Dura.


###### Rashi on Habakkuk 1:4:2

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/1:4:2](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/1:4:2)


**וְלֹא יֵצֵא לָנֶצַח מִשְׁפָּט.** הַדָּת לַאֲמִתּוֹ.


**and justice does not go out forever** I.e., the true decision of the Law.


###### Rashi on Habakkuk 1:4:3

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/1:4:3](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/1:4:3)


**מַכְתִּיר.** סוֹבֵב, כְּמוֹ (תהילים כב:יג) ״אַבִּירֵי בָשָׁן כִּתְּרוּנִי״.


**surrounds** surrounds, as in (Ps. 22:13): “Strong bulls of Bashan have surrounded me (כִּתְּרוּנִי).”



### Verse 5

###### Rashi on Habakkuk 1:5:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/1:5:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/1:5:1)


**לֹא תַאֲמִינוּ כִּי יְסֻפָּר.** זֹאת לָכֶם אֲשֶׁר אֲנִי מֵקִים אֶת הַכַּשְׂדִּים, הָעָם אֲשֶׁר לֹא הָיָה כְּדַאי לְהִבָּרְאוֹת, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר (ישעיהו כג:יג) ״זֶה הָעָם לֹא הָיָה״. הֵם אֶחָד מִשְּׁלֹשָׁה דְבָרִים שֶׁהַקָּדוֹשׁ בָּרוּךְ הוּא כִּבְיָכוֹל מִתְחָרֵט עַל בְּרִיאָתָם, וְעַתָּה יָקוּם וְיִהְיֶה מַר וְנִמְהָר לָרוּץ וְלָלֶכֶת לְמֶרְחֲבֵי אַרְצוֹת כָּל הַגּוֹיִם.


**You shall not believe when it is told** When this is told to you, that I am setting up the Chaldeans, the people that did not deserve to be created, as it is written (Isa. 23:13): “This people has never been.” They are one of three things whose creation God regrets, and now [they] will rise, and [they] will be bitter, and [they will be] impetuous to run and to march to the breadth of the lands of all the nations.



### Verse 7

###### Rashi on Habakkuk 1:7:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/1:7:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/1:7:1)


**מִמֶּנּוּ מִשְׁפָּטוֹ וּשְׂאֵתוֹ יֵצֵא.** מִמֶּנּוּ שׁוֹפְטִים, מִמֶּנּוּ מְלָכִים הַמְּטִילִין מַשָּׂא וְאֵימָה עַל הַכֹּל. **מִשְׁפָּטִים** – יושטיצי״א בְּלַעַ״ז.


**from themselves their judgment and their burden shall emanate** From them are judges; from them are kings, who cast a burden and a fear upon all. **their judgment** *joustice* in O.F:

###### Rashi on Habakkuk 1:8:1

### Verse 9

###### Rashi on Habakkuk 1:9:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/1:9:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/1:9:1)


**לְחָמָס יָבוֹא.** לִשְׁלֹל וְלָבֹז.


**come to commit violence** to plunder and to spoil.


###### Rashi on Habakkuk 1:9:2

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/1:9:2](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/1:9:2)


**מְגַמַּת פְּנֵיהֶם.** לְשׁוֹן ״הַגְמִיאִינִי נָא״ (בראשית כד:יז), ״יְגַמֶּא אָרֶץ״ (איוב לט:כד). רָץ בִּמְרוּצָה לְשָׁעָה קַלָּה כִּבְרַת אֶרֶץ, כְּאִלּוּ גָּמָא וְשָׁתָה הָאָרֶץ שֶׁלְּפָנָיו. וְאַף כָּאן ״מְגַמַּת פְּנֵיהֶם״, גְּמִיאַת שְׁאִיפַת פְּנֵיהֶם דּוֹמֶה לְרוּחַ הַקָּדִים עַזָּה שֶׁבָּרוּחוֹת, כֵּן תִּרְגֵּם יוֹנָתָן.


**the eagerness of their faces** an expression like (Gen. 24: 17) “Give me to swallow, (הַגְמִיאִינִי) I pray you” and (Job 39:24) “He swallows (יְגַמֶּה) the earth.” He runs swiftly - in a short time a distance of a day’s plowing - as though he had swallowed and drunk the earth that is before him. Here, too, the swallowing of their faces; the swallowing, the longing of their faces resembles the east wind, the fiercest of the winds. So did *Jonathan* render this.



### Verse 10

###### Rashi on Habakkuk 1:10:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/1:10:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/1:10:1)


**וְהוּא בַּמְּלָכִים יִתְקַלָּס.** מִתְלוֹצֵץ בָּהֶם, וְכָל לְשׁוֹן ׳קַלָּסָה׳ לְשׁוֹן ׳דִּבּוּר׳ הַנִּדְבָּרִים בּוֹ, יֵשׁ לְטוֹב יֵשׁ לְרַע, פרלרי״ץ בְּלַעַ״ז.


**And they shall mock the kings** shall mock them. Every expression of קַלָּסָה is an expression for the speech of those speaking about another; some [of these speeches] are for good, and some for bad - *parledic* in O.F.


###### Rashi on Habakkuk 1:10:2

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/1:10:2](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/1:10:2)


**מִשְׂחָק.** כְּמוֹ ׳שְׂחוֹק׳, כְּמוֹ ׳מִשְׁמָר׳, ׳מִסְתָּר׳.


**a laughingstock** like שְּׂחוֹק, laughter, like מִשְׁמָר, a watch, and מִסְתָּר, a hiding place.


###### Rashi on Habakkuk 1:10:3

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/1:10:3](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/1:10:3)


**לְכָל מִבְצָר יִשְׂחָק.** אִם אוֹיְבוֹ בְּמִבְצָר חָזָק, יִשְׂחַק לוֹ.


**He shall scoff at every fortress** If his enemy is in a strong fortress, he scoffs at him.


###### Rashi on Habakkuk 1:10:4

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/1:10:4](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/1:10:4)


**וַיִּצְבֹּר עָפָר.** עָלָיו בְּרֹב חֵילוֹתָיו. כָּל אֶחָד נוֹשֵׂא מַשָּׂא עָפָר, וַהֲרֵי הִיא לִפְנֵי הַחוֹמָה תֵּל גָּבוֹהַּ וְנִלְחָמִים בָּהּ עַל הָעִיר, וְהִיא שְׁפִיכַת סוֹלְלָה, כִּי כְֻּלָּן מְתֻרְגָּמוֹת ״וַיִּצְבֹּר עֲלָהּ מְלֵיתָא״, וְאַף הִיא לְשׁוֹן ׳מִלּוּא׳ שֶׁהָיָה בְּעִיר דָּוִד. הוּא תֵּל גָּבוֹהַּ וְנִלְחָמִים בָּהּ, שֶׁעַל רֹאשָׁהּ מִגְדָּל בָּנוּי וְשִׁפּוּעוֹ מְשַׁפֵּעַ לַצְּדָדִין, וּסְבִיבוֹת מַרְגְּלוֹתָיו חוֹמָה נְמוּכָה לְהַחֲזִיק הֶעָפָר מִלִּפֹּל.


**he piles up earth** upon it with his many armies, each one carrying a load of earth. It [the pile] is before the wall as a high mound, and they [the Chaldeans] battle from upon it. This is called שְׁפִיכַת סוֹלְלָה, pouring a siege mound (cf. Isa. 37:33), for all of them are translated into Aramaic: וְיִצְבּוֹר עֲלָה מְלֵיתָא, and he piled upon it a filling. This is an expression of Milo, for in the city of David there was a high mound, and they would battle upon it. Atop the mound was built a tower whose slant was inclined to the sides, and around its base was a low wall to prevent the earth from falling.



### Verse 11

###### Rashi on Habakkuk 1:11:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/1:11:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/1:11:1)


**אָז חָלַף רוּחַ.** אָז בִּרְאוֹתוֹ דַּרְכּוֹ צָלְחָה, רוּחַ חוֹלֶפֶת וְעוֹבֶרֶת עָלָיו, רוּחַ אַשְׁמָה. וּמַהוּ הָרוּחַ? זֶה **כֹחוֹ לֵאלֹהוֹ** – הַכֹּחַ הַזֶּה הוּא נוֹתֵן לֵאלֹהוֹ, וְאָמַר: אֱלֹהַי עָשָׂה לִי כָּל הַחַיִל הַזֶּה.


**Then a spirit goes further** Then, when they see their way prospering, a spirit of guilt goes further and passes over them. Now what is the spirit? **this strength they attribute to their god** This strength they attribute to their god, and they say, “My god acquired all this wealth for me.” Says the prophet, “Now, You, why should You remain silent to all this?”



### Verse 12

###### Rashi on Habakkuk 1:12:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/1:12:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/1:12:1)


אָמַר הַנָּבִיא: וְאַתָּה לָמָּה תַחֲרִישׁ לְכָל זֹאת? **הֲלֹא אַתָּה מִקֶּדֶם אֱלֹהַי קְדוֹשִׁי וְכוּ׳**. וְזֶה שֶׁכָּתוּב ״לֹא נָמוּת״ – אֶחָד מִתִּקּוּנֵי סוֹפְרִים שֶׁבַּמִּקְרָא הוּא, שֶׁכִּנָּה הַכָּתוּב. וְכֵן (מלאכי א:יג) ״וְהִפַּחְתֶּם אוֹתוֹ״, וְכֵן הַרְבֵּה הַמְפֹרָשִׁים בְּסִפְרֵי. וּלְפִי תִּיוּן הַסּוֹפְרִים זֶה, פֵּרוּשׁוֹ: הֲלֹא אַתָּה אֱלֹהַי מִקֶּדֶם קְדוֹשִׁי? אַל תִּתְּנֵנִי לָמוּת בְּיָדוֹ.


**Are You not from everlasting, O Lord, my God, my Holy One?** Who shall not die. Now, the reason it is written לֹא נָמוּת we shall not die, is that it is one of the emendations of the scribes in Scripture, by which Scripture euphemizes. Likewise, (Mal. 1:13) “And you sadden it.” And so are many of them [these euphemisms] explained in *Sifre* (Num. 10:35). According to the emendation of the scribes, this is its explanation: Are you not my God from everlasting, my Holy One? Do not deliver us into their hands to die.

**and it shall not fail; though it tarry** it shall not fail to come, though that appointed time may take long to come. **wait for it for...; it shall not delay** at all after the seventy years. This clause can also be explained to mean: though he tarry, meaning the prophet Jeremiah. *Jonathan* renders: the prophecy is written and explained in the Book of the Torah. He translated כְּתֹב as כָּתוּב. It has already been alluded to in the Torah (Lev. 26:34): “Then shall the land placate [God concerning] its Sabbaths.” Israel sinned by violating seventy Sabbatical Years, in which they did not release the land, and, corresponding to them, they were exiled therefrom for seventy years. And so it is stated in II Chronicles (36:21): “Until the land placated its Sabbaths; all the days it lay waste, it rested to complete seventy years.” And so you find in Ezekiel (4:5): “And I have given you the years of their iniquity according to the number of days, three hundred and ninety days... (verse 6) I gave you a day for a year.” And Scripture says (verse 4): “And you lie on your left side, etc.” You are found saying that Israel provoked God for three hundred and ninety years until the Ten Tribes were exiled, and the tribe of Judah sinned for forty years, and from the time the Ten Tribes were exiled until the destruction of Jerusalem are the twenty-two years of Manasseh. The rest of Manasseh’s years were spent in repentance, for it is stated concerning him (II Kings 21:2f.) “And he did what was evil, etc.,... as Ahab... had made.” Therefore we count Manasseh’s evil years according to the number of Ahab’s years, and he reigned for twenty-two years. With two years of his son Amon’s reign, and eleven of Jehoiakim’s, and eleven of Zedekiah’s, the evil years total forty-six, and this prophecy was said to Ezekiel in the fifth year of Zedekiah. In any case, we find the time of their sinning four hundred and thirty years, for after this prophecy they tarried there six years. [I.e., the two tribes of Judah and Benjamin remained in the land for six more years] In four hundred years, there are eight Jubilee Years, and in every Jubilee cycle there are seven Release Years, making a total of fifty-six Release Years and eight years consecrated as Jubilee Years making all together sixty-four. In thirty-six years there are five Release Years, totaling sixty-nine consecrated years, and this final Jubilee year is also counted in the number, since it was not completed because of their iniquity. And here, this is what the Holy One, blessed be He, said to Habakkuk: A vision is already written in the Torah, but it is sealed. You write and explain on the tablets, for a vision of this appointed time will yet be revealed.

This is the wicked Nebuchadnezzar, who would give the kings wine to drink, intoxicate them, and practice pederasty upon them, as we state in Tractate *Shabbath* (149b). Another explanation. **Woe to him who gives his friend to drink** In Seder Olam, it [this verse] is expounded regarding Belshazzar, who gave the princes to drink with the vessels of the Temple, because of which they were smitten with zaraath and intoxicated by the wrath of the Holy One, blessed be He. On that night he [Belshazzar] was slain.

**נָקַבְתָּ בְמַטָּיו רֹאשׁ.** סַנְחֵרִיב וְסִיעָתוֹ שֶׁהָיוּ סוֹעֲרִים בְּרוּחַ סְעָרָה לַהֲפִיצֵנִי, וְהוּא הָיָה מַטֶּה שֶׁהָיִיתָ רוֹדֶה בּוֹ אֶת הָעוֹבְדֵי כּוֹכָבִים וּמַזָּלוֹת. וּכְשֶׁבָּא וְסָעַר לַהֲפִיצֵנִי, נָקַבְתָּ רָאשֵׁי גֵּיסוֹתָיו בְּמַטָּיו שֶׁהָיָה בָּא לִרְדּוֹת בָּהֶם.


**You pierced the heads... with war clubs** Sennacherib and his company. **they storm** Who were storming with a tempest to scatter me. [Sennacherib] was the staff with which you chastised the nations. When he came and stormed to scatter me, You pierced the heads of his troops with his staffs, with which he had come to chastise me.


###### Rashi on Habakkuk 3:14:2

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/3:14:2](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/3:14:2)


**רֹאשׁ פְּרָזָיו.** רָאשֵׁי עָרָיו וְטִירוֹתָיו, כְּמוֹ ״פְּרָזוֹת תֵּשֵׁב יְרוּשָׁלַיִם״ (זכריה ב:ח), ״עָרֵי הַפְּרָזִי״ (דברים ג:ה).


**the heads of his villages** The heads of his towns and his castles, as in (Deut. 3:5) “The open towns” and (Zech. 2:8) “Jerusalem shall be inhabited without walls.”


###### Rashi on Habakkuk 3:14:3

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/3:14:3](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/3:14:3)


**עֲלִיצֻתָם.** הָיוּ, כְּשֶׁהָיוּ יְכוֹלִים לֶאֱכֹל עָנִי בַּמִּסְתָּר. יִשְׂרָאֵל הַקְּרוּאִים עַם עָנִי.


**their joy** was when they could... **devour a poor one in secret** Israel, known as a poor people.



### Verse 15

###### Rashi on Habakkuk 3:15:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/3:15:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/3:15:1)


**דָּרַכְתָּ בַיָּם.** בְּחֵילוֹתָיו הַכְּבֵדִים כְּחוֹל הַיָּם.


**You trampled in the sea** You trampled upon [Sennacherib’s] hordes, which were as heavy as the sand by the sea.


###### Rashi on Habakkuk 3:15:2

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/3:15:2](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/3:15:2)


**חֹמֶר.** תִּרְגֵּם יוֹנָתָן: בְּדִגּוּר. לְשׁוֹן ״חֳמָרִים חֳמָרִים״ (שמות ח:י).


**a heap of many waters** *Jonathan* renders. upon a heap, an expression of (Exod. 8: 10) “many heaps.”



### Verse 16

###### Rashi on Habakkuk 3:16:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/3:16:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/3:16:1)


**שָׁמַעְתִּי וַתִּרְגַּז בִּטְנִי.** תִּרְגֵּם יוֹנָתָן: ״אֲמַרַת בָּבֶל: שְׁמָעֵית וְזָעוּ מַלְכַיָּא מִן קֳדָם דִּינָא דִּי אִתְדָּנוּ מִצְרָאֵי״.


**I heard, and my inward parts trembled** *Jonathan* rendered. Said Babylon, “I heard, and the kings trembled before the judgement meted out upon the Egyptians.”


###### Rashi on Habakkuk 3:16:2

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/3:16:2](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/3:16:2)


**לְקוֹל צָלֲלוּ שְׂפָתַי.** לְקוֹל הַשְּׁמוּעָה אֲחָזַנִי רַעַד, עַד שֶׁשְּׂפָתַי נוֹקְשׁוֹת זוֹ לָזוֹ וְקוֹלוֹ נִשְׁמָע.


**my lips quivered at the sound** At the sound of the report, trembling took hold of me until my lips knocked one against the other and their sound was heard.


###### Rashi on Habakkuk 3:16:3

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/3:16:3](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/3:16:3)


**צָלֲלוּ.** לְשׁוֹן ״מְצִילּוֹת הַסּוּס״ (זכריה יד:כ), טינטי״ר בְּלַעַ״ז.


**quivered** An expression of (Zech. 14:20) “The bells of the horses.” *Tentir* in O.F., to tinkle.

**וְתַחְתַּי אֶרְגָּז.** בִּמְקוֹמִי אֲנִי מִזְדַּעְזַעַת.


**and I quaked in my place** In my place, I quake.


###### Rashi on Habakkuk 3:16:5

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/3:16:5](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/3:16:5)


**אֲשֶׁר אָנוּחַ לְיוֹם צָרָה.** אֲשֶׁר מְנוּחָתִי זֹאת מוּכֶנֶת לְיוֹם צָרָה.


**that [the time] I would rest is destined for a day of trouble** That this tranquility of mine is destined for a day of trouble.


###### Rashi on Habakkuk 3:16:6

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/3:16:6](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/3:16:6)


**לַעֲלוֹת לְעַם יְגוּדֶנּוּ.** לְיוֹם אֲשֶׁר אָמַר לְהַעֲלוֹת מִתּוֹכוֹ אֶת הָעָם אֲשֶׁר הוּא יְגוּדֶנּוּ עָקֵב, לָשׁוּב בִּגְדוּדָיו לְאַרְצוֹ.


**to bring up a people that will troop back** For the day that He said to bring up from there the people that He will cause to troop back, to return with its troops to its land.



### Verse 17

###### Rashi on Habakkuk 3:17:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/3:17:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/3:17:1)


**כִּי תְאֵנָה לֹא תִפְרָח.** כְּתַרְגּוּמוֹ, וְיֵשׁ לְפָתְרוֹ כְּמַשְׁמָעוֹ: שׁוּב אֵין הַצְלָחָה לְכָל מַעֲשֵׂה בָּבֶל מֵעַתָּה.


**For a fig tree shall not blossom** As the Targum renders. However, the phrase may be interpreted according to its simple meaning: From now on, none of Babylon’s deeds shall succeed.

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/3:17:2](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/3:17:2)


**וּשְׁדֵמוֹת.** שְׂדֵה לָבָן.


**the grain field** a white field.


###### Rashi on Habakkuk 3:17:3

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/3:17:3](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/3:17:3)


**מִמִּכְלָה.** דִּיר הַצֹּאן.


**from the fold** a stall for sheep.



### Verse 18

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/3:18:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/3:18:1)


**וַאֲנִי.** כְּנֶסֶת יִשְׂרָאֵל, בַּה׳ אֶעֱלוֹזָה.


**Yet I** the nation of Israel, will rejoice in the Lord.



### Verse 19

###### Rashi on Habakkuk 3:19:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/3:19:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/3:19:1)


**לַמְנַצֵּחַ בִּנְגִינוֹתַי.** לְבֶן לֵוִי הַמְּנַצֵּחַ בַּשִּׁיר בְּבֵית הַמִּקְדָּשׁ, אַנְעִים לוֹ בִּנְגִינוֹתַי, וְהַלֵּוִי יִתֵּן קוֹל כְּנֶגְדּוֹ בִּכְלֵי הַשִּׁיר.


**To the conductor [to play] with my melodies** To the Levite who conducts the music in the Temple. I will compose for him [the Levites’ conductor] with my melodies, and the Levite[s] will accompany him with musical instruments.


###### Rashi on Habakkuk 3:19:2

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/3:19:2](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/3:19:2)


**לַמְנַצֵּחַ.** כְּמָה דְאַתְּ אָמַר (עזרא ג:ח) ״וַיַּעֲמִידוּ הַלְוִיִּם מִבֶּן עֶשְׂרִים שָׁנָה וָמַעְלָה לְנַצֵּחַ עַל מְלֶאכֶת וְגוֹמֵר״.


**To the conductor** As it is stated (Ezra 3:8) “... appointed the Levites from twenty years old and upward to superintend the work of the house of the Lord.”


###### Rashi on Habakkuk 3:19:3

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/3:19:3](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Habakkuk/r/3:19:3)


**בִּנְגִינוֹתַי.** הוּא לְשׁוֹן נְעִימוֹת קוֹל לְהָרִים וּלְהַשְׁפִּיל, אורגנידור״ש בְּלַעַ״ז.


**with my melodies** This is an expression of a vocal melody to raise and lower, *orgenedors* in O.F.
