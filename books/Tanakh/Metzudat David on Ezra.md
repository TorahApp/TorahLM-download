# Metzudat David on Ezra

מצודת דוד על עזרא

Link: [https://torahapp.org/share/book/Metzudat%20David%20on%20Ezra](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Metzudat%20David%20on%20Ezra)

18th-century classic commentary on Prophets and Writings explaining verses in clear and simple language, primarily based on the Radak’s commentary.
