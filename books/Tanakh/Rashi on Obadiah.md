## Chapter 1


### Verse 1

###### Rashi on Obadiah 1:1:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Obadiah/r/1:1:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Obadiah/r/1:1:1)


**חֲזוֹן עֹבַדְיָה.** מַאי שְׁנָא עֹבַדְיָה לֶאֱדוֹם וְלֹא נִיבָּא נְבוּאָה אַחֶרֶת? אָמְרוּ חֲכָמִים: עֹבַדְיָה גֵּר אֲדוֹמִי הָיָה. אָמַר הַקָּדוֹשׁ בָּרוּךְ הוּא: מֵהֶם וּבָהֶם אָבִיא עֲלֵיהֶם. יָבֹא עֹבַדְיָה שֶׁדָּר בֵּין שְׁנֵי רְשָׁעִים, אַחְאָב וְאִיזֶבֶל, וְלֹא לָמַד מִמַּעֲשֵׂיהֶם, וְיִפְרַע מֵעֵשָׂו הָרָשָׁע שֶׁדָּר בֵּין שְׁנֵי צַדִּיקִים, יִצְחָק וְרִבְקָה, וְלֹא לָמַד מִמַּעֲשֵׂיהֶם:


**The vision of Obadiah**—Why is Obadiah different that he was chosen to prophesy concerning Edom and did not prophesy any other prophecy? Our Sages of blessed memory stated: Obadiah was an Edomite proselyte. Said the Holy One, blessed be He: From them and in them will I bring upon them. Let Obadiah, who dwelt between two wicked people, Ahab and Jezebel, and did not learn from their deeds, come and impose retribution upon Esau, who dwelt between two righteous people, Isaac and Rebecca, and did not learn from their deeds. [from *Sanhedrin* 39b]



### Verse 2

###### Rashi on Obadiah 1:2:1

**הִנֵּה קָטֹן נְתַתִּיךָ.** כְּלַפֵּי שֶׁקְּרָאוֹ אָבִיו בְּנוֹ הַגָּדוֹל וְאִמּוֹ בְּנָהּ הַגָּדוֹל, אָמַר הַקָּדוֹשׁ בָּרוּךְ הוּא: לְפָנַי קָטֹן הוּא. רַבּוֹתֵינוּ דָּרְשׁוּ: קָטֹן - שֶׁלֹּא הָיָה לָהֶם לֹא כְּתָב וְלֹא לָשׁוֹן:


**Behold I have made you small**—In contrast with what his father called him, his big son, and his mother called him her big son, the Holy One, blessed be He, says: In My eyes, he is small (*Pesikta d’Rav Kahana* p. 56; *Pesikta Rabbathi*, ed. Meir Ish Shalom p. 79a; *Pesikta Rabbathi* ed. Warsaw p. 144; *Gen. Rabbah* 65:11). And our Sages (*Avodah Zarah* 10a) expounded: small for they have neither script nor language. [from *Avodah Zarah* 10a]

**בָּזוּי.** שֶׁלֹּא הָיוּ מַעֲמִידִין מֶלֶךְ בֶּן מֶלֶךְ:

### Verse 3

###### Rashi on Obadiah 1:3:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Obadiah/r/1:3:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Obadiah/r/1:3:1)


**שֹׁכְנִי בְחַגְוֵי סֶלַע.** סוֹמֵךְ עַל מִשְׁעֶנֶת אֲבוֹתָיו אַבְרָהָם וְיִצְחָק, וְהֵם לֹא יוֹעִילוּ לוֹ. חַגְוֵי כְּמוֹ "וְהָיְתָה אַדְמַת יְהוּדָה לְמִצְרַיִם לְחָגָּא" (ישעיהו יט:יז), ברייטיינ"א בְּלַעַ"ז. וְאַל תִּתְמַהּ עַל וָי"ו שֶׁל חַגְוֵי שֶׁהִיא כְּוָי"ו שֶׁל קְצָוֵי (תהלים סה:ו) וּכְוָי"ו שֶׁל כִּמְטַחֲוֵי (בראשית כא:טז), שֶׁאֵין יְסוֹד בַּתֵּיבָה אֶלָּא טֵי"ת וְחֵי"ת:


**who dwell in the clefts of the rock**—Who relies on the support of his ancestors, Abraham and Isaac, but they shall not avail him. **the clefts** Heb. חַגְוֵי. Cf. (Isa. 19:17) “And the land of Judah shall be to Egypt for a dread (לְחָגָּא),” *frayteyne* [or *fraite*] in O.F., a cleft of a rock. And do not wonder about the “vav” of (Gen. 21:16) “like a shot (כִּמְטַחֲוֵי) ;” in which there is no radical in the word but the “teth” and the “heth.”

**אֵיךְ נִדְמֵיתָה.** לָמָּה נִרְדַּמְתָּה דוּמָם עַד שֶׁגָּנְבוּ כָּל רְצוֹנָם:


**How were you silent?**—Why were you sleeping soundly and still until they stole all they desired?


###### Rashi on Obadiah 1:5:3

**הֲלֹא בּוֹצְרִים יַשְׁאִירוּ עוֹלֵלוֹת.** וְאֵלֶּה לֹא יַשְׁאִירוּ לְךָ כְּלוּם, כִּי יַחְפְּשׂוּךָ וִיגַלּוּ וְיִדְרְשׁוּ מַצְפּוּנֶיךָ:


**would (the vintagers) not leave over some gleaning grapes?**—But these will not leave you anything, for they will search and reveal and seek out your hidden things.



### Verse 6

###### Rashi on Obadiah 1:6:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Obadiah/r/1:6:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Obadiah/r/1:6:1)


**נִבְעוּ.** תַּרְגּוּם יוֹנָתָן: אִיתְגַּלְיָין, וּלְשׁוֹן אֲרָמִית נִבְעוּ נִתְבַּקְּשׁוּ, וְכֵן "אִם תִּבְעָיוּן בְּעָיוּ" (ישעיהו כא:יב):


**were revealed** Heb. נִבְעוּ. *Jonathan* rendered: were revealed, and it is an Aramaic expression: נִבְעוּ means “sought out.” Cf. (Isa. 21: 12) “If you will request, request (אִם תִּבְעָיוּן בְּעָיוּ).”

###### Rashi on Obadiah 1:7:1

### Verse 12

###### Rashi on Obadiah 1:12:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Obadiah/r/1:12:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Obadiah/r/1:12:1)


**וְאַל תֵּרֶא בְיוֹם אָחִיךָ.** לֹא הָיָה לְךָ לְהַבִּיט וְלַעֲמוֹד מִנֶּגֶד:


**And you should not have looked on the day of your brother**—You should not have looked and stood from afar.


###### Rashi on Obadiah 1:12:2

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Obadiah/r/1:12:2](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Obadiah/r/1:12:2)


**בְּיוֹם נָכְרוֹ.** בְּיוֹם הִסְגִּירוֹ בְּיַד אוֹיֵב, הוּא הַבַּבְלִיִּים. וְכֵן הוּא אוֹמֵר בְּשָׁאוּל "נִכַּר אֹתוֹ אֱלֹהִים בְּיָדִי" (שמואל א כג:ז), הִסְגִּיר אוֹתוֹ בְּיָדִי:


**on the day of his being delivered** Heb. נָכְרוֹ. On the day of his being delivered into the hands of the heathens. And so does Scripture state concerning Saul: (I Sam. 23:7) “נִכַּר אֹתוֹ אֱלֹהִים בְּיָדְי,” “He has delivered him into my hand.”



### Verse 13

###### Rashi on Obadiah 1:13:1

**וְאַל תִּשְׁלַחְנָה בְחֵילוֹ.** אַל תִּשְׁלַחְנָה יָדְךָ בִּנְכָסָיו, כֵּן תִּרְגֵּם יוֹנָתָן. וּמִקְרָא קָצָר הוּא וְצָרִיךְ לְהוֹסִיף עָלָיו יָדְךָ:


**and you should not have stretched out upon their possessions**—You should not have stretched out your hand upon their possessions. So did *Jonathan* render. And this is an ellipsis, to which we must add: “your hands.”



### Verse 14

###### Rashi on Obadiah 1:14:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Obadiah/r/1:14:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Obadiah/r/1:14:1)


**וְאַל תַּעֲמֹד עַל הַפֶּרֶק.** מָקוֹם שֶׁהַבּוֹרְחִים יוֹצְאִים דֶּרֶךְ שָׁם לִימָּלֵט, וּבִלְשׁוֹן לַעַ"ז קוֹרְאִים אוֹתוֹ טרו"ג:


**And you should not have stood by the gap** Heb. הַפֶּרֶק. The place through which the escapees emerge to escape, and in French they call it “trou.” **And you should not have stood by me gap**—From here [we learn] that one may not interrupt between one paragraph and another in reading the “Shma.” [*Haggadah of Rabbi Akiva*]

###### Rashi on Obadiah 1:16:1

**כִּי ה' דִּבֵּר.** וְהֵיכָן דִּבֵּר? "וְיֵרְדְּ מִיַּעֲקֹב וְהֶאֱבִיד שָׂרִיד וְגוֹ'" (במדבר כד:יט):


**for the Lord has spoken**—Now where did He speak? (Num. 24:19) “Out of Jacob shall come a ruler, and he shall destroy him that remains in the city.” [from *Mechilta Bo* 12:16, *Pirké d’Rabbi Eliezer* ch. 37]



### Verse 19

###### Rashi on Obadiah 1:19:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Obadiah/r/1:19:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Obadiah/r/1:19:1)


**וְיָרְשׁוּ הַנֶּגֶב.** יִשְׂרָאֵל שֶׁהָיוּ יוֹשְׁבִין בִּדְרוֹמָהּ שֶׁל אֶרֶץ יִשְׂרָאֵל יִירְשׁוּ אֶת הַר עֵשָׂו שֶׁהוּא בְּמֵצַר דָּרוֹם, וּבְנֵי הַשְּׁפֵלָה יִירְשׁוּ אֶת אֶרֶץ פְּלִשְׁתִּים וְאֶת הַר אֶפְרַיִם וְאֶת הַר שׁוֹמְרוֹן:


**And [the inhabitants of] the southland shall inherit**—Israel, who were dwelling in the south of Eretz Israel, shall inherit the mountain of Esau, which is at the southern boundary; and the people of the plain shall inherit the land of the Philistines and Mt. Ephraim and Mt. Shomeron.

**וּבִנְיָמִין אֶת הַגִּלְעָד.** וּבְנֵי מְנַשֶּׁה שֶׁהָיְתָה אֶרֶץ גִּלְעָד שֶׁלָּהֶם יִפְשְׁטוּ לָהֶם מֵהַלְאָה לִגְבוּלוֹתֶיהָ שֶׁל אֶרֶץ יִשְׂרָאֵל בְּמִזְרָחָהּ:


**and Benjamin with [the inhabitants of] Gilead** And the children of Manasseh, whose territory was the land of Gilead, shall spread beyond the borders of Eretz Israel on the east.



### Verse 20

###### Rashi on Obadiah 1:20:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Obadiah/r/1:20:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Obadiah/r/1:20:1)


**וְגָלוּת הַחֵל הַזֶּה.** תִּרְגֵּם יוֹנָתָן: עַמָּא הָדֵין. הַחֵל לְשׁוֹן חַיִל, כְּמוֹ "וַיָּבֹא יְרוּשָׁלַיִם בְּחֵיל כָּבֵד" (ישעיהו לו:ב) דְּרַבְשָׁקֵה, אֶלָּא שֶׁזֶּה חָסֵר יוֹ"ד. וְיֵשׁ עוֹד לְפָרֵשׁ הַחֵל הַזֶּה - גָּלוּת הַגַּיְא הַזֶּה:


**And this exiled host** Heb. הַחֵל. *Jonathan* renders: This people. הַחֵל, An expression of a host. Cf. (Isa. 36:2) “And he came (sic) to Jerusalem with an army (חֵיל) of a great multitude,” which deals with Rabshakeh, only that this one is missing a “yud.” It is also possible to explain גָלֻת הַחֵל as “the exile of this valley.”


###### Rashi on Obadiah 1:20:2

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Obadiah/r/1:20:2](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Obadiah/r/1:20:2)


**לִבְנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל אֲשֶׁר כְּנַעֲנִים עַד צָרְפַת.** גָּלוּת אֲשֶׁר הוּא מִבְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל שֶׁגָּלוּ מֵעֲשֶׂרֶת הַשְּׁבָטִים לְאֶרֶץ כְּנַעֲנִים עַד צָרְפַת:


**who are [with] the Canaanites as far as Zarephath**—The exile which is of the children of Israel who were exiled from the ten tribes to the land of the Canaanites as far as Zarephath


###### Rashi on Obadiah 1:20:3

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Obadiah/r/1:20:3](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Obadiah/r/1:20:3)


**וְגָלוּת יְרוּשָׁלַיִם אֲשֶׁר בִּסְפָרַד.** שֶׁהֵם מִבְּנֵי יְהוּדָה אֲשֶׁר גָּלוּ לִסְפָרַד, הֵם יִירְשׁוּ אֶת עָרֵי הַנֶּגֶב שֶׁבִּדְרוֹמָהּ שֶׁל אֶרֶץ יִשְׂרָאֵל. וְאוֹמְרִים הַפּוֹתְרִים: **צָרְפַת** הוּא הַמַּלְכוּת שֶׁקּוֹרִין פרנצ"א בְּלַעַ"ז. **סְפָרַד** תִּרְגֵּם יוֹנָתָן: אספמיא:


**and the exile of Jerusalem which is in Sepharad**—who are of the people of Judah who were exiled to Sepharad - they shall inherit the cities of the southland, which are in the southern part of Eretz Israel. The exegetes claim that Zarephath is the kingdom called France in French. **Sepharad**—*Jonathan*renders: Spain.



### Verse 21

###### Rashi on Obadiah 1:21:1

**וְעָלוּ.** שָׂרֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל מוֹשִׁיעִים בְּהַר צִיּוֹן:


**shall ascend**—Princes of Israel as saviors on Mt. Zion.


###### Rashi on Obadiah 1:21:2

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Obadiah/r/1:21:2](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Obadiah/r/1:21:2)


**לִשְׁפֹּט אֶת הַר עֵשָׂו.** לִיפָּרַע מֵהַר עֵשָׂו אֲשֶׁר עָשׂוּ לְיִשְׂרָאֵל. לִשְׁפֹּט יושטיציי"ר בְּלַעַ"ז:


**to judge the mountain of Esau**—to exact retribution from the mountain of Esau for what they did to Israel. **to judge** Heb. לִשְׁפֹּט. *joustiser* in O.F.

**הַר עֵשָׂו.** תַּרְגּוּם יוֹנָתָן: כַּרְכָּא רַבָּא דְעֵשָׂו:


**the mountain of Esau**—*Jonathan* renders: the great city of Esau.

**וְהָיְתָה לַה' הַמְּלוּכָה.** לִמֶּדְךָ שֶׁאֵין מַלְכוּתוֹ שְׁלֵימָה עַד שֶׁיִּפָּרַע מֵעֲמָלֵק:


**and the Lord shall have the kingdom**—This teaches you that His kingdom will not be complete until He exacts retribution from Amalek.
