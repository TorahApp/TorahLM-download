# Metzudat David on Nahum

מצודת דוד על נחום

Link: [https://torahapp.org/share/book/Metzudat%20David%20on%20Nahum](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Metzudat%20David%20on%20Nahum)

18th-century classic commentary on Prophets and Writings explaining verses in clear and simple language, primarily based on the Radak’s commentary.
