## Chapter 1


### Verse 1

###### Rashi on Nahum 1:1:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/1:1:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/1:1:1)


**מַשָּׂא נִינְוֵה.** תִּרְגֵּם יוֹנָתָן ״מַטַּל כָּס דִּלְוָוט לְאַשְׁקָאָה יָת נִינְוֵה״.


**The harsh prophecy concerning Nineveh** Heb. מַשָׂא. The burden of the cup of the curse [which was] to be given Nineveh to drink.


###### Rashi on Nahum 1:1:2

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/1:1:2](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/1:1:2)


**סֵפֶר חֲזוֹן נַחוּם.** חָזוֹן נָקוּד קָמָץ לְפִי שֶׁאֵינוֹ דָבוּק וְאֵינוֹ דוֹמֶה לַחֲזוֹן יְשַׁעְיָהוּ שֶׁהוּא נָקוּד חֲטַף פַּתַּח, וְכֵן פֵּרוּשׁ סֵפֶר חֲזוֹן כְּבָר נִכְתַּב עָלֶיהָ, וְהוּא נְבוּאַת יוֹנָה בֶּן אֲמִתַּי וְעַתָּה חָזַר וְנִתְנַבֵּא עָלֶיהָ נַחוּם הָאֶלְקוֹשִׁי אֶת הַמַּשָּׂא הַזֶּה. **אֶלְקוֹשׁ** הָיָה שֵׁם עִירוֹ, וְכֵן תִּרְגֵּם יוֹנָתָן: ״מִלְּקַדְמִין אִתְנַבִּי עֲלָהּ יוֹנָה בַּר אַמְתַּי וְתָבַת מֵחוֹבָהָא, וְכַדּוּ דְּאוֹסִיפַת לְמֵיחְטֵי תָּב וְאִתְנַבִּי עֲלָהּ נַחוּם דְּמִבֵּית אֶלְקוֹשׁ״.


**The book of the vision of Nahum the Elkoshite**—חזון is vowelized with a “kamatz” (חָזוֹן) since it is not in the construct state, and it is unlike “חֲזוֹן יִשַׁעְיָהוּ,” the vision of Isaiah, which is vowelized with a “hataf pattah.” This is its meaning: A book of vision has already been written concerning it [Nineveh], the prophecy of Jonah son of Amittai; and now, again, Nahum the Elkoshite prophesied this harsh prophecy over it. Elkosh is the name of his [Nahum’s] city. And so did *Jonathan* paraphrase: In early times, Jonah son of Amittai prophesied concerning it, and they repented of their sins, and when they continued to sin, Nahum of the house of Elkosh prophesied further concerning them. **the Elkoshite**—That city is in the province of Ballynia, which is in the state of Eretz Israel, although it is outside the Holy Land. Proof of the matter is that there is gold, silver, and salt dust near it because the Dead Sea, which is near Eretz Israel, goes there under the earth. In this state they do not crown a king the son of a king [i.e., the throne is not hereditary]; and they are of the seed of Judah. [*Sod Mesharim*]



### Verse 2

###### Rashi on Nahum 1:2:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/1:2:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/1:2:1)


**אֵל קַנּוֹא וְנוֹקֵם ה׳.** עַל זַרְעוֹ שֶׁל סַנְחֵרִיב נִתְנַבֵּא נַחוּם וּבִימֵי מְנַשֶּׁה, כְּמוֹ שֶׁמָּצִינוּ בְּסֵדֶר עוֹלָם.


**The Lord is a jealous and vengeful God**—Nahum prophesied concerning Sennacherib’s descendants, and in the time of Manasseh, as we find in *Seder Olalm* (ch.20).


###### Rashi on Nahum 1:2:2

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/1:2:2](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/1:2:2)


**אֵל קַנָּא וְנוֹקֵם ה׳.** מֵאָז שֶׁנִּפְרַע לְיִשְׂרָאֵל מֵאוֹיְבֵיהֶם וְגַם עֲדַיִן עַתָּה נוֹקֵם הוּא וּבַעַל חֵמָה וְעָתִיד הוּא לִהְיוֹת נוֹקֵם לְצָרָיו שֶׁהֶחֱרִיבוּ אֶת אַרְצוֹ וְהִגְלוּ אֶת עַמּוֹ וְנוֹטֵר הוּא אֶת אֵיבָתוֹ לַבַּבְלִיִּים. וְר״שׁ אוֹמֵר שָׁלוֹשׁ נְקִימוֹת הַלָּלוּ כְּנֶגֶד שָׁלוֹשׁ גְלִיּוֹת שֶׁהִגְלָה סַנְחֵרִיב אֶת יִשְׂרָאֵל, כְּמוֹ שֶׁמָּצִינוּ בְּסֵדֶר עוֹלָם.


**The Lord is a jealous and vengeful God**—from the time He avenged Israel upon their enemies, and now too, He is still vengeful and full of wrath. **He is destined to wreak vengeance upon His adversaries** who destroyed their land and exiled His people. **and he bears a grudge** Heb. וְנוֹטֵר, lit. watches. He lays up His hatred for His enemies. Rabbi Simon says: These three expressions of vengeance correspond to the three exiles to which Sennacherib exiled Israel, as we find in *Seder Olam* (ch. 23).



### Verse 3

###### Rashi on Nahum 1:3:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/1:3:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/1:3:1)


**ה׳ אֶרֶךְ אַפַּיִם וּגְדָל כֹּחַ.** גְּדָל כֹּחַ הוּא וְהַיְכֹלֶת בְּיָדוֹ לְהִנָּקֵם וַאֲשֶׁר לֹא מִהֵר נִקְמָתוֹ לְפִי שֶׁאֶרֶךְ אַפַּיִם הוּא וּמִכָּל מָקוֹם **נַקֵּה לֹא יְנַקֶּה**.


**The Lord is slow to anger and great in power**—He is great in power, and He has the ability to wreak vengeance. The reason He did not hasten His revenge is that He is slow to anger, but, in any case, He will not acquit.


###### Rashi on Nahum 1:3:2

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/1:3:2](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/1:3:2)


**ה׳ בְּסוּפָה וּבִשְׂעָרָה דַּרְכּוֹ.** הֵם שְׁלוּחָיו לְהִפָּרַע מֵאוֹיְבָיו, שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר בְּמִצְרַיִם (שמות יד:כא) ״בְּרוּחַ קָדִים עַזָּה״, וּבְדוֹר הַמַּבּוּל (איוב ד:ט) ״מִנִּשְׁמַת אֱלוֹהַּ יֹאבֵדוּ״, בְּצֹר נֶאֱמַר (יחזקאל כו:כו) ״רוּחַ קָדִים שִׁבְּרֵךְ״.


**the Lord - His way is with a tempest and with a storm**—They [the tempest and storm] are His messengers for wreaking retribution upon His enemies, as it is stated concerning Egypt (Exod. 14:21): “with a mighty east wind”; and concerning the generation of the Flood (Job 4:9): “From the breath of God they perish.” Concerning Tyre, it is stated (Ezek. 27:26): “The east wind has broken you.”


###### Rashi on Nahum 1:3:3

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/1:3:3](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/1:3:3)


**וְעָנָן אֲבַק רַגְלָיו.** גַּם הוּא דֶּרֶךְ נִקְמָתוֹ, (שמות יד:כד) ״וַיַּשְׁקֵף אֶל מַחֲנֵה מִצְרַיִם בְּעַמּוּד אֵשׁ וְעָנָן״, (יחזקאל ל:יח) ״הִיא עָנָן יְכַסֶּנָּה״.


**and cloud is the dust of His feet**—That, too, is the way of His vengeance. (Exod. 14:24) “And the Lord looked over the camp of the Egyptians with a pillar of fire and cloud”; also, (Ezek. 30:18) “As for her, a cloud shall cover her.”



### Verse 4

###### Rashi on Nahum 1:4:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/1:4:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/1:4:1)


**גּוֹעֵר בַּיָּם.** כְּמַשְׁמָעוֹ, וְדֻגְמָא הוּא כְּלַפֵּי הָעוֹבְדֵי כּוֹכָבִים וּמַזָּלוֹת שֶׁנִּמְשְׁלוּ לַמַּיִם, כָּעִנְיָן שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר (ישעיהו יז:יב) ״לְאֻמִּים כִּשְׁאוֹן מַיִם כַּבִּירִים יִשָּׁאוּן״.


**He rebukes the sea**—To be understood [both] according to its apparent meaning, and as a figure describing the nations, who are compared to water, as the matter is stated (Isa. 17:12): “Like the rushing of mighty waters they rush.”


###### Rashi on Nahum 1:4:2

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/1:4:2](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/1:4:2)


**וְכָל הַנְּהָרוֹת הֶחֱרִיב.** כָּאן נִבָּא שֶׁעָתִיד הַקָּבָּ״ה לְהַמְלִיךְ אֶת נְבוּכַדְנֶצַּר (ירמיה כה) בִּימֵי יְהוֹיָקִים וְיִתֵּן אֶת אַשּׁוּר וְאֶת כָּל הָאֲרָצוֹת לַחֶרֶב.


**and He has dried up all the rivers**—Here he prophesies that the Holy One, blessed be He, is destined to crown Nebuchadnezzar in the days of Jehoiakim, and He will deliver Assyria and all the lands to the sword.


###### Rashi on Nahum 1:4:3

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/1:4:3](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/1:4:3)


**אֻמְלַל בָּשָׁן וְכַרְמֶל.** מוֹשָׁבוֹת הַטּוֹבִים.


**Bashan and Carmel**—The good dwelling places.


###### Rashi on Nahum 1:4:4

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/1:4:4](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/1:4:4)


**וּפֶרַח לְבָנוֹן אֻמְלָל.** חַכְמֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל פֵּרְשׁוּ כְּלַפֵּי מִינֵי מְגָדִים שֶׁנָּטַע שְׁלֹמֹה בְּבֵית הַמִּקְדָּשׁ שֶׁהָיוּ שֶׁל זָהָב, וְכֵיוָן שֶׁנִּכְנְסוּ גּוֹיִם לַהֵיכָל יָבְשׁוּ.


**and the blossoms of the Lebanon are cut off**—The Sages of Israel explained this as an allusion to the various types of sweet fruit that Solomon planted in the Temple, which were gold; and as soon as the gentiles entered the Temple, they withered. [from *Yoma* 21b, 39b]



### Verse 5

###### Rashi on Nahum 1:5:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/1:5:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/1:5:1)


**הָרִים רָעֲשׁוּ מִמֶּנּוּ.** הַמְּלָכִים וְהַשָּׂרִים מֵהַכַּשְׂדִּים יִפְחֲדוּ מִגְּזֵרָתוֹ.


**Mountains quaked because of Him**—The kings and the princes (of the heathens) shall fear His decree.

**raised up** Heb. וַתִּשָׂא ; i.e., raised up a pillar of smoke.



### Verse 6

###### Rashi on Nahum 1:6:1

**נִתְּכָה.** הִגִּיעַ לָאָרֶץ, כְּמוֹ (שמות ט:לג) ״וּמָטָר לֹא נִתַּךְ אָרְצָה״.


**has reached** Heb. נִתְּכָה, has reached the earth, as in (Exod. 9:33) “And rain did not reach (נְתַּךְ) the earth.”


###### Rashi on Nahum 1:6:2

### Verse 7

###### Rashi on Nahum 1:7:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/1:7:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/1:7:1)


**טוֹב ה׳ לְמָעוֹז.** וְאַף בְּשָׁעָה שֶׁהוּא נִפְרָע מֵאוֹיְבָיו לֹא זָזוּ רַחֲמָיו מִלְּהֵיטִיב לִירֵאָיו, וְלֹא כְּמִדַּת בָּשָׂר וָדָם שֶׁכְּשֶׁהוּא עָסוּק בָּזוֹ אֵינוֹ פָּנוּי בָּזוֹ, כָּעִנְיָן שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר (שמות טו:ג) ״ה׳ אִישׁ מִלְחָמָה ה׳ שְׁמוֹ״, כְּמוֹ שֶׁהוּא בִּמְכִלְתִּין.


**The Lord is good yea, a stronghold**—Even when he inflicts retribution upon His enemies, His mercies do not cease to do good to those who fear Him. [This ability is] unlike the trait of [a man of] flesh and blood, who, when he is busy with one thing, is not free to [simultaneously] do another thing, as the matter is stated (Exod. 15:3): “The Lord is a man of war; the Lord is His Name.” [This is] as it is stated in *Mechilta* (ad loc.)


###### Rashi on Nahum 1:7:2

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/1:7:2](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/1:7:2)


**וְיֹדֵעַ.** אֶת צָרְכֵי חוֹסֵי בוֹ.


**and is cognizant of**—the necessities of those who trust in Him.



### Verse 8

###### Rashi on Nahum 1:8:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/1:8:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/1:8:1)


**וּבְשֶׁטֶף עֹבֵר.** וּבְחֵמָה שׁוֹטֶפֶת.


**But, with an overrunning flood**—with flooding wrath.


###### Rashi on Nahum 1:8:2

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/1:8:2](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/1:8:2)


**כָּלָה יַעֲשֶׂה מְקוֹמָהּ.** שֶׁל אֶרֶץ, וּמוּסָב זֶה עַל ״וַתִּשָּׂא הָאָרֶץ מִפָּנָיו״ כְּמוֹ שֶׁאָמוּר לְמַעְלָה.


**He shall make a full end of its place**—the place of the land. This refers back to “and the land raised up from before Him,” as is stated above.



### Verse 9

###### Rashi on Nahum 1:9:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/1:9:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/1:9:1)


**כָּלָה הוּא עֹשֶׂה.** לְנִינְוֵה וּמַלְכֵי אַשּׁוּר לֹא יִשְׁנֶה לָהֶם עוֹד פַּעַם אַחֶרֶת.


**He will make a full end**—of Nineveh and the kings of Assyria. He will not repeat this to you again.



### Verse 10

###### Rashi on Nahum 1:10:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/1:10:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/1:10:1)


**כִּי עַד סִירִים סְבֻכִים.** כְּלוֹמַר פִּתְאֹם תָּבֹא עֲלֵיהֶם מַכָּתָם וְלֹא יֵרְדוּ מִכְּבוֹדָם מְעַט מְעַט, כִּי בְּעוֹד שֶׁהַסִּירִים וְהַקּוֹצִים נִסְבָּכִים נִשְׁרָשִׁים בְּחָזְקָה.


**For, while the thorns are entangled**—I.e., their destruction will come upon them suddenly, and they will not descend from their honor gradually, but while the thorns and the briers are [yet] entangled; i.e., strongly rooted.

**כַּיֶּלֶק.** הַגּוֹמֵר וּמְכַלֶּה כָּל יֶרֶק הַשָּׂדֶה, וְיֵשׁ פּוֹתְרִין ״הִתְכַּבֵּד כַּיֶּלֶק״ – תִּתְכַּסִּי בְּכֹבֶד גְּיָסוֹת כְּיֶלֶק, וְ״הִתְכַּבֵּד״ לְשׁוֹן עַם כָּבֵד.


**like the nibbling locust**—which finishes and destroys completely all the vegetation of the field. Some interpret: -Heb. הִתְכַּבֵּד כַּיָּלֶק. You shall be covered heavily with troops like the nibbling locust, wherein הִתְכַּבֵּד is an expression of a heavy people (עַם כֶּבֶד).

### Verse 17

###### Rashi on Nahum 3:17:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/3:17:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Nahum/r/3:17:1)


**מִנְּזָרַיִךְ.** שָׂרַיִךְ, וְהַמֵּ״ם יְסוֹד וְשֹׁרֶשׁ בַּתֵּיבָה, כְּמֵ״ם שֶׁל מִשְׁמַרְתְּךָ וְשֶׁל מִשְׁמַעְתֶּךָ וְשֶׁל מִנְעָלֶיךָ, וּפְעָמִים שֶׁהִיא נוֹפֶלֶת מִמֶּנָּה, כְּמוֹ ״נְזִיר אֶחָיו״ (בראשית מט:כו), וְכֵן ״נְזִירֶיהָ שָׂרֶיהָ״ (איכה ד:ז).


**Your princes** Heb. מִנְּזָרַיִךְ, your princes. The “mem” is a radical and a root of the word, like the “mem” of מִשְׁמַרְתְּךָ, your watch (Num. 18:3); of מִשְׁמַעְתֶּךָ, your service (I Sam. 22:14); and of מִנְעָלֶךָ, your lock (Deut. 33:25). But sometimes the “mem” is defective; e.g., נְזִיר אֶחָיו, the prince of his brothers (Gen. 49:26); and, similarly, לִנְזָרִים, princes (Amos 2:11).
