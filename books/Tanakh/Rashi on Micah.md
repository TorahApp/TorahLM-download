### Verse 4

###### Rashi on Micah 1:4:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/1:4:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/1:4:1)


**כַּדּוֹנַג.** שַׁעֲוָה:


**like wax** Heb. כַּדּוֹנַג. In mishnaic Hebrew, this is שַּׁעֲוָה


###### Rashi on Micah 1:4:2

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/1:4:2](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/1:4:2)


**מֻגָּרִים בְּמוֹרָד.** תִּרְגֵּם יוֹנָתָן: ״דְּאֲשִׁידִין בְּמוֹדְרוֹן״, מֻגָּרִים לְשׁוֹן ׳זִיבַת מַיִם׳ וְכֵן ״עֵינִי נִגְּרָה״ (איכה ג:מט), ״נִגָּרוֹת בְּיוֹם אַפּוֹ״ (איוב כ:כח), ״פָּרַץ נַחַל מֵעִם גָּר״ (שם כח), ״וַיַּגֵּר מִזֶּה״ (תהלים עה:ט):


**as water poured down a steep place** Heb. מֻגָּרִים. *Jonathan* renders: poured down a steep place. מְגֻרִים is an expression of the running of water. Cf. (Lam. 3:49) “My eye trickles down (נִגְרָה)”; (Job 20:28) “Shall flow away (נִגָרוֹת) on the day of His wrath”; (ibid. 28:4) “He breaks open a water course from the place it flows (גָּר)”; (Ps. 75:9) “And He pours out (וַיַגֵּר) of this.”



### Verse 6

###### Rashi on Micah 1:6:1

###### Rashi on Micah 1:7:2

### Verse 8

###### Rashi on Micah 1:8:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/1:8:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/1:8:1)


**שׁוֹלָל.** מִשְׁתַּגֵּעַ כְּשׁוֹטֶה בְּתִמְהוֹן לֵב, כְּמוֹ ״אֶשְׁתּוֹלְלוּ אַבִּירֵי לֵב״ (תהלים עו), ״וְסָר מֵרָע מִשְׁתּוֹלֵל״ (ישעיהו נט) וְהַתָּי״ו הִיא בָּאָה לְשַׁמֵּשׁ בַּתֵּיבָה בִּלְשׁוֹן מִתְפַּעֵל וְנִתְפַּעֵל, וְכֵן הִלּוּךְ תֵּיבָה שֶׁתְּחִלַּת יְסוֹדָהּ שִׁי״ן אוֹ סָמֶ״ךְ כְּשֶׁבָּאוּ עָלָיו תָּי״ו בִּלְשׁוֹן מִתְפַּעֵל וְנִתְפַּעֵל נִכְנֶסֶת תּוֹךְ הַתֵּיבָה כְּמוֹ ׳מִשְׁתּוֹלֵל׳, אוֹמֵר אֲנִי שֶׁהוּא מִלְּשׁוֹן שׁוֹגֵג כְּמוֹ ״לֹא תַשְׁלֶה אוֹתִי״ (מלכים ב ד:כח), ״עַל הַשַּׁל״ (שמואל ב ו:ז), ״דִּי יֵאמַר שָׁלוּ״ (דניאל ג):


**I will go mad** Heb. שׁוֹלָל, becoming mad like a mentally deranged person, with bewilderment. Cf. (Ps. 76:6) “The stout-hearted have become mad (אֱשְׁתּוֹלְלוּ)”; (Isa. 59:15) “And he who turns away from evil is considered mad (מִשְׁתּוֹלֵל).” The ‘tav’ comes into the word to serve in the reflexive present and past. This is the normal pattern of a word whose first radical letter is ‘shin’ or ‘sammech’ when a ‘tav’ is added in the reflexive, either present or past, as in מִשְׁתּוֹלֵל. I say that it is an expression of error, as in (II Kings 4:28) “Do not mislead (תַשְׁלֶה) me”; (II Sam. 6:7) “For his error (הַשַּׁל) ;” (Dan. 3: 29) “who speaks anything amiss (שָׁלוּ).”

###### Rashi on Micah 1:9:1

**כִּי אֲנוּשָׁה מַכּוֹתֶיהָ.** כִּי חוֹלָה הִיא בְּמַכּוֹתֶיהָ, כְּמוֹ ״וַיֵּאָנַשׁ״ (שמואל ב יב):

### Verse 10

###### Rashi on Micah 1:10:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/1:10:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/1:10:1)


**בְּגַת אַל תַּגִּידוּ.** פֶּן יִשְׂמְחוּ לָנוּ פְּלִשְׁתִּים:


**Do not declare it in Gath**—lest the Philistines rejoice about us.


###### Rashi on Micah 1:10:2

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/1:10:2](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/1:10:2)


**בְּבֵית לְעַפְרָה.** עָפְרַת אֲבִי הָעֶזְרִי:


**in the houses of Aphrah**—This is Ophrah of the Abiezrites (Jud. 6:11).


###### Rashi on Micah 1:10:3

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/1:10:3](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/1:10:3)


**עָפָר הִתְפַּלָּשִׁי.** לָשׁוֹן נוֹפֵל עַל הַלָּשׁוֹן וְכֵן יוֹשֶׁבֶת שָׁפִיר עֶרְיָה בֹּשֶׁת וְכֵן לֹא יָצְאָה יוֹשֶׁבֶת צַאֲנָן כֻּלָּם לְפִי שֵׁם הָעִיר לְשׁוֹן הַקִּינָה. **הִתְפַּלָּשִׁי** הִתְגַּלְגְּלִי. פִּתְרוֹנוֹ לְפִי עִנְיָנוֹ הִתְעַפְּרִי:


**wallow in the dust** Heb. הִתְפַּלָּשִׁי, a play on words with the name Philistia (פְּלֶשֶת). Similarly, (verse 11) “You inhabitant of Shafir, with your private parts exposed.” Similarly, (verse 11) “The inhabitant of Zanaan has not gone forth.” In all of these, the language of the lamentation is according to the name of the city. **wallow** Heb. הִתְפַּלָּשִׁי, roll. According to the context, cover yourself with dust.



### Verse 11

###### Rashi on Micah 1:11:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/1:11:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/1:11:1)


**עִבְרִי לָכֶם.** הִתְחִיל בִּלְשׁוֹן יְחִידוּת נְקֵבָה כִּמְדַבֵּר אֶל כְּנֵסִיָּה הָעִיר כְּמוֹ שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר יוֹשֶׁבֶת שָׁפִיר כֻּלָּם בְּשֵׁם כְּנֵסִיָּה אַחַת וְאָמַר לָכֶם כִּמְדַבֵּר אֶל הָרַבִּים שֶׁהֲרֵי לָרַבִּים הוּא אוֹמֵר:


**Pass along your way** Heb. עִבְרִי לָכֶם. He commences with an expression in the feminine singular, as used in addressing the people of the city, as it is said: “the inhabitant of Shafir” addressing them all in the name of one people. And then he says, “לָכֶם, along your way” as used in addressing many people, for he was speaking to the public.

**מִסְפַּד בֵּית הָאֵצֶל יִקַּח מִכֶּם עֲמָדְתוֹ.** הַמִּסְפֵּד שֶׁגְּרַמְתֶּם לַנִּגְזָלִים שֶׁאַתֶּם גּוֹזְלִים נַחֲלָתוֹ כְּדֵי לְהַגִּישׁ בַּיִת בְּבַיִת שָׂדֶה בְּשָׂדֶה זֶה אֵצֶל זֶה, הוּא יִקַּח מִכֶּם עֲמָדְתוֹ מַעֲמַד הַבִּנְיָנִים שֶׁבְּנִיתֶם וְהֶעֱמַדְתֶּם עַל נַחֲלָתָם, בֵּית הָאֵצֶל איוטמנ״ט בְּלַעַ״ז:


**the lamentation caused by drawing the houses one to the other shall take its stand from you**—The lamentation you caused those who were robbed, whom you robbed of their inheritance in order to draw one house to the other, one field to the other, will take its stand from you - the stand of the houses that you built and erected on their inheritance. **by drawing the houses from one the other** ajoustement in Old French [which means, adding or joining].



### Verse 12

###### Rashi on Micah 1:12:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/1:12:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/1:12:1)


**כִּי חָלָה לְטוֹב.** כִּי קִוְּתָה לְטוֹבָה אוֹתָהּ הַיּוֹשֶׁבֶת בְּשַׁלְוָתָהּ וּמַמְרָה עַל פִּי הַנְּבִיאִים:


**For the one dwelling in disobedience hoped for good**—For the one dwelling in tranquility and disobeying the word of the prophets hoped for good.


###### Rashi on Micah 1:12:2

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/1:12:2](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/1:12:2)


**כִּי יָרַד רָע.** הֲרֵי כִּי זֶה מְשַׁמֵּשׁ בִּלְשׁוֹן אֶלָּא הִיא חָלָה לְטוֹב אֲבָל מִפְּנֵי מְרוּתָהּ יָרַד לָהּ רָע:


**but evil descended** Heb. כִּי. This instance of כִּי serves as an expression of “but.” She hoped for good, but, because of her disobedience, evil descended to her.



### Verse 13

###### Rashi on Micah 1:13:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/1:13:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/1:13:1)


**רְתֹם הַמֶּרְכָּבָה לָרֶכֶשׁ.** אִסְרוּ הַמֶּרְכָּבָה לָרֶכֶשׁ שֶׁהוּא קַל לָרוּץ וְתִבְרְחוּ. וְאָמַר דּוּנַשׁ שֶׁהוּא לְשׁוֹן עַרְבִי:


**Hitch the chariot to the swift steeds** Heb. רְתֹם הַמֶּרְכָּבָה לַרֶכֶשׁ. Hitch the chariot to the רֶכֶשׁ, which is swift to run, and you shall escape. The word רֶכֶשׁ is explained by Dunash (*Teshuvoth Dunash* p. 69) as an Arabic word.

###### Rashi on Micah 1:14:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/1:14:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/1:14:1)


**לָכֵן תִּתְּנִי שִׁלּוּחִים עַל מוֹרֶשֶׁת גַּת.** עַל אֲשֶׁר תִּתְּנִי שִׁלּוּחִים עַל מַלְכוּת דָּוִד אֲשֶׁר הוֹרִישׁ אֶתְכֶם גַּת כְּעִנְיָן שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר (דברי הימים א יח) ״וַיִּקַּח דָּוִד אֶת גַּת מִיַּד פְּלִשְׁתִּים״, וְאַתֶּם מוֹאֲסִים בּוֹ כְּמוֹ שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר (ישעיהו ח) ״יַעַן אֲשֶׁר מָאַס הָעָם הַזֶּה אֶת מֵי הַשִּׁלֹחַ וְגוֹמֵר״, לְפִיכָךְ בָּתֵּי אַכְזִיב שֶׁהָיְתָה מֵאֶרֶץ יְהוּדָה וְנִמְשְׁכוּ אַחַר פֶּקַח בֶּן רְמַלְיָהוּ, סוֹפָן לִהְיוֹת לְאַכְזָב לְמַלְכֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל שֶׁיֵּהָרֵג פֶּקַח בֶּן רְמַלְיָהוּ וְיִכְזְבוּ הַנִּסְמָכִים עָלָיו. וּבִימֵי אָחָז נִתְנַבֵּאת נְבוּאָה זוֹ:


**Since you give expulsions to the dynasty that gave you the possession of Gath**—Since you give expulsions to the kingdom of David, which gave you the possession of Gath, as the matter is stated (I Chron. 18:1): “And he took Gath and its cities out of the hands of the Philistines.” But you reject it, as it is stated (Isa. 8:6): “Since this people has rejected the waters of the Shiloah that flow gently, etc.” Therefore, “the houses of Achzib” which was of the land of Judah and was drawn after Pekah the son of Remaliah, shall ultimately be a “dried up spring” to the kings of Israel - for Pekah the son of Remaliah will be assassinated, and all those who relied on him will be disappointed. This prophecy was prophesied during Ahaz’s reign.

**לָכֵן תִּתְּנִי שִׁלּוּחִים.** פֵּרוּשׁ לָכֵן כְּמוֹ עַל אֲשֶׁר כְּמוֹ (ישעיהו יז) ״עַל כֵּן תִּטְּעִי נִטְעֵי נַעֲמָנִים״ עַל אֲשֶׁר נְטַעְתִּיךְ נִטְעֵי נַעֲמָנִים וְאַתְּ נֶהְפַּכְתְּ לִזְמוֹרוֹת זָר:


**since you give expulsions** Heb. לָכֵן. The definition of לָכֵן is עַל אֲשֶׁר, because of. Cf. (Isa. 17:10) “Because (עַל כֵּן) I planted you with pleasant saplings,” and you turned into a branch of a stranger.

**up to Adullam**—(which is within the border) will the glory of Israel retreat and come, for the enemies shall conquer from their border up to Adullam. So did *Jonathan* render: Until Adullam shall they go up and enter the border of the land of Israel. And I heard a Midrash Aggadah in the name of Rabbi Menachem: **I will yet bring the heir**—The children of Elam were fit to inherit the land of Israel - for he [Elam] was the firstborn of the sons of Shem - but because of love for your forefathers I transferred it to the children of Arphachshad. Now I will bring the Chaldeans and the Elamites upon them.

### Verse 4

###### Rashi on Micah 2:4:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/2:4:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/2:4:1)


**וְנָהָה נְהִי נִהְיָה.** וְנָהָה לְשׁוֹן עָתִיד, כְּמוֹ וְעָשָׂה וּפָנָה. וְנָהָה הַמְּקוֹנֵן וְאָמַר נְהִי נִהְיָה בָּעוֹלָם וּמַהוּ הַנְּהִי אֲשֶׁר **נִהְיָה אָמַר שָׁדוֹד נְשַׁדֻּנוּ** אוֹיְבֵינוּ שְׁדָדוּנוּ. וְלָשׁוֹן **נְשַׁדֻּנוּ** שֶׁכָּתַב בּוֹ נוּ״ן בִּתְחִלָּה וְלֹא כָתַב שַׁדּוּנוּ, מִפְּנֵי שֶׁבָּא לְשַׁמֵּשׁ שְׁנֵי לְשׁוֹנוֹת, לְשׁוֹן נִפְעַלְנוּ וְלָשׁוֹן פְּעַלְנוּ. אִלּוּ כָּתַב נִשְׁדַּנוּ הָיָה הַלָּשׁוֹן מוּסָב עַל הַנִּשְׁדָּדִים וְלֹא אֶל הַשּׁוֹדְדִים, וְאִם כָּתַב שַׁדּוּנוּ הָיָה מוּסָב אֶל הַשּׁוֹדְדִים וְלֹא לַנִּשְׁדָּדִים, עַכְשָׁיו מוּסָב לִשְׁנֵיהֶם נְתָנָנוּ בְּיַד אֲשֶׁר שַׁדּוּנוּ וּבְלַעַ״ז שומי״ר דימשטיור״א נשו״ט. דָּבָר אַחֵר **נְשַׁדֻּנוּ** אֲנַחְנוּ גָּרַמְנוּ לְעַצְמֵנוּ שֶׁשְּׁדָדוּנוּ:


**and he shall lament, “A lamentation has come about…”** Heb. וְנָהָה נְהִי נִהְיָה. וְנָהָה is a future tense, like וְעָשָה, and he shall make, and וּפָנָה, and he shall turn. The lamenting one shall lament and say, “A lamentation has come about in the world. Now what is the lamentation that has come about?...” **They have robbed us!** Heb. שָׁדוֹד נְשַׁדֻּנוּ. Our enemies have robbed us, The prophet uses the expression נְשַׁדֻּנוּ, in which there is a “nun” in the beginning, and does not use שַׁדוּנוּ, because נְשַׁדֻּנוּ serves in two voices, the passive voice and the active voice. Had the prophet written נְשַדֹנוּ, in the passive voice, the expression would refer to the robbed and not to the robbers. Had he written שַדוּנוּ, in the active voice, the expression would refer to the robbers and not to the robbed. This way, it refers to both: we were given into the hand of those who robbed us. In Old French, there is the expression *somes degatert nous*, we were ravaged. Another explanation: נְשַׁדֻּנוּ means we brought upon ourselves that they robbed us.

**אֵיךְ יָמִישׁ לִי לְשׁוֹבֵב שָׂדֵינוּ יְחַלֵּק.** אֵיךְ יָסוּר אֵלַי עוֹד לְהָשִׁיב לָנוּ שְׂדוֹתֵינוּ אֲשֶׁר יְחַלֵּק אוֹתָם הָעוֹבֵד כּוֹכָבִים וּמַזָּלוֹת הַזֶּה לוֹ, אֵיךְ אֶפְשָׁר לְשׁוֹבְבָם עוֹד לִי. כָּל אֵלֶּה דִּבְרֵי הַמְּקוֹנֵן וְנוֹשֵׂא הַמָּשָׁל. **אֵיךְ יָמִישׁ לִי** קומ״א שדיטורנר״א אמו״י כְּמוֹ סוּרָה אֵלַי וַיָּסַר אֵלָיו:


**How will He turn to me to return our fields, which he divides**—How will He turn again to me, to return our fields to us, which this enemy divides for himself? How is it possible to return them to me anymore? All these are the words of the lamenting one who takes up the parable. **How will He turn to me**—*Kome se destornera amois*? As in (Jud. 4:18) “Turn to me.... And he turned to her.”

###### Rashi on Micah 2:6:2

**לֹא יִסַּג כְּלִמּוֹת.** פֶּן יַשִּׂיגוּן אֶתְכֶם כְּלִימוֹת מֵאִתָּם כְּעִנְיָן שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר (משלי ט:ח) ״אַל תּוֹכַח לֵץ״, כָּל טִיף לְשׁוֹן ׳נְבוּאָה׳ כְּמוֹ (עמוס ז:טז) ״וְלֹא תַטִּיף עַל בֵּית יִשְׂחָק״, (יחזקאל כא:ב) ״וְהַטֵּף אֶל דָּרוֹם״:

**בַּעֲבוּר טָמְאָה תְּחַבֵּל וְחֶבֶל נִמְרָץ.** כִּי לְמַעַן טַמֵּא אוֹתָהּ הִיא עוֹשָׂה חֲבוּרַת חֶבְלֵי רְשָׁעִים, **תְּחַבֵּל** לְשׁוֹן ׳אֲגֻדּוֹת׳ כְּמוֹ (שמואל א י:ה) ״חֶבֶל נְבִיאִים״ (תהלים קיט:סא) ״חֶבְלֵי רְשָׁעִים״ וְכֵן תִּרְגֵּם יוֹנָתָן: ״מִסְתַּיְּעִין עֲלָהּ״, לְשׁוֹן ׳סִיעָה׳:

The word “hidden” is derived from אֹפֶל, darkness.] But it is possible to interpret מִגְדַּל עֵדֶר as the Temple, and עֹפֶל as an expression of a mighty tower. **וְאַתָּה בֵּית לֶחֶם אֶפְרָתָה.** שֶׁיָּצָא דָוִד מִשָּׁם שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר בּוֹ (שמואל א יז:נח) ״עַבְדְּךָ יִשַׁי בֵּית הַלַּחְמִי״ וּבֵית לֶחֶם קָרוּי אֶפְרָת שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר (בראשית מח:ז) ״בְּדֶרֶךְ אֶפְרָת הִיא בֵּית לָחֶם״:


**And you, Bethlehem Ephrathah**—whence David emanated, as it is stated (I Sam. 17:58): “The son of your bondsman, Jesse the Bethlehemite.” And Bethlehem is called Ephrath, as it is said (Gen. 48: 7): “On the road to Ephrath, that is Bethlehem.”

### Verse 2

###### Rashi on Micah 5:2:1

**לָכֵן יִתְּנֵם עַד עֵת יוֹלֵדָה.** יִתְּנֵם בְּיַד אוֹיְבֵיהֶם עַד בֹּא הָעֵת אֲשֶׁר חָלָה וְיָלְדָה צִיּוֹן אֶת בָּנֶיהָ, צִיּוֹן אֲשֶׁר הֶחֱזִיקָה עַתָּה חִיל כַּיּוֹלֵדָה וְעַכְשָׁיו הִיא קְרוּיָה יוֹלֶדֶת. וְרַבּוֹתֵינוּ אָמְרוּ מִכָּאן אֵין בֶּן דָּוִד בָּא עַד שֶׁתִּפְשׁוֹט הַמַּלְכוּת הָרְשָׁעָה תִּשְׁעָה חֳדָשִׁים עַל כָּל הָעוֹלָם כֻּלּוֹ, אַךְ לְפִי פְשׁוּטוֹ זֶהוּ הַצָּעָה שֶׁפֵּרַשְׁתִּי:


**Therefore, He shall deliver them until the time a woman in confinement gives birth**—He shall deliver them into the hands of their enemies until the coming of the time that Zion has felt the pangs of labor and borne her children; Zion, which is now seized by the pangs of labor, is now called a woman in confinement. [I.e., now the labor pains will cease and the redemption will come about.] But our Sages state that from here we deduce that the son of David will not come until the wicked kingdom spreads over the entire world for nine months (*Yoma* 10b, *Sanh.* 98b). But, according to its simple meaning, this is the structure as I explained.

This is vowelized with a “chataf kamatz” because it is not a verb in the present tense, like:, יוֹשֵב, sits, and אוֹמֵר, says; rather, it is a gerund, as in (Isa. 33:4): “The gathering of (אֹסֶף) the locusts”; like the gathering of קַיִץ. These are the last figs, which are inferior. And so did *Jonathan* render: as the late figs of the summer.

### Verse 14

###### Rashi on Micah 7:14:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/7:14:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/7:14:1)


**שֹׁכְנִי לְבָדָד.** שֶׁיְּהֵא שׁוֹכֵן בֶּטַח בָּדָד.


**who dwell alone**—That they should dwell alone, in security.


###### Rashi on Micah 7:14:2

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/7:14:2](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/7:14:2)


**יַעַר בְּתוֹךְ כַּרְמֶל.** תִּרְגֵּם יוֹנָתָן: ״דַּהֲווֹ צָדִין בְּחוּרְשָׁא יִתְיַתְבוּן כְּכַרְמְלָא״, אוֹתָן שֶׁהָיוּ שׁוֹמְמִים כִּיעָרִוֹת יִשְׁכְּנוּ לְבָדָד בְּתוֹךְ כַּרְמֶל שֶׁהוּא מָקוֹם מְיֻשָּׁב. וְיֵשׁ לְפָרֵשׁ **שֹׁכְנִי לְבָדָד** בַּיְּעָרִים לֹא יִרְאוּ מֵחַיּוֹת הַשָּׂדֶה כִּבְתוֹךְ כַּרְמֶל.


**a forest in the midst of a fruitful field**—*Jonathan* renders: Those who were desolate in a forest shall settle in the “karmel”; those who were desolate in the forests shall dwell alone in the midst of the “karmel,” which is a settled place. It is also possible to explain: **who dwell alone**—In the forests will not fear the wild beasts as [they would not] in the midst of a fruitful field.



### Verse 17

###### Rashi on Micah 7:17:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/7:17:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/7:17:1)


**כְּזוֹחֲלֵי אָרֶץ.** הֵם נְחָשִׁים הַזּוֹחֲלִים עַל גְּחוֹנָם בַּעֲפַר הָאָרֶץ.


**as those who crawl on the earth**—They are snakes, which crawl on their bellies on the dust of the earth.


###### Rashi on Micah 7:17:2

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/7:17:2](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/7:17:2)


**יִרְגְּזוּ מִמִּסְגְּרוֹתֵיהֶם.** יֶחֶרְדוּ מֵרֹב דֹּחַק אִסּוּרֵיהֶם וּמַסְגֵּר כִּלְאָם.


**They shall quake from their imprisonment**—They shall quake because of the extreme narrowness of their imprisonment and the confinement of their captivity.



### Verse 20

###### Rashi on Micah 7:20:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/7:20:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/7:20:1)


**תִּתֵּן אֱמֶת לְיַעֲקֹב.** תִּרְגֵּם יוֹנָתָן: ״תִּתְיְהַב קוּשְׁטֵיהּ דְּיַעֲקֹב לִבְנוֹהִי כְּמָה דְקַיָּמְתָּא לֵיהּ בְּבֵית אֵל טָבוּת אַבְרָהָם לְזַרְעֵיהּ בַּתְרוֹהִי כְּמָה דְקַיָּמְתָּא לֵיהּ בֵּין בִּתְרַיָּא תִּדְכַּר לָנָא עֲקֵדַת יִצְחָק״, תִּתֵּן לָנוּ בֶּאֱמֶת אֲשֶׁר הִבְטַחְתָּ לְיַעֲקֹב הָאֱמֶת לָנוּ דְּבָרֶיךָ אֲשֶׁר הִבְטַחְתָּ לְיַעֲקֹב ״כִּי לֹא אֶעֱזָבְךָ״ (בראשית כ״ט).


**You shall give the truth of Jacob**—*Jonathan* paraphrases: You shall give the truth of Jacob to his sons, as You swore to him in Bethel; the loving-kindness of Abraham to his seed after him, as you swore to him ‘between the parts.’ You shall remember for us the binding of Isaac, etc. Give us the truth that You promised Jacob. Cause to come true Your word that You promised Jacob (Gen. 28: 15): “For I will not forsake you.”


###### Rashi on Micah 7:20:2

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/7:20:2](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/7:20:2)


**חֶסֶד לְאַבְרָהָם.** שְׂכַר חֶסֶד אֲשֶׁר לְאַבְרָהָם אֲשֶׁר צִוָּה לְבָנָיו ״לִשְׁמֹר דֶּרֶךְ ה׳ לַעֲשׂוֹת צְדָקָה וּמִשְׁפָּט״, וּלְכָךְ לֹא נֶאֱמַר וְחֶסֶד אֶלָּא חֶסֶד הָאֱמֶת שֶׁתְּאַמֵּת לָנוּ אֶת הַבְטָחַת יַעֲקֹב הִיא תִּהְיֶה תַשְׁלוּם גְּמוּל חַסְדּוֹ שֶׁל אַבְרָהָם.


**the loving-kindness of Abraham** The reward for the loving- kindness of Abraham, [out of] which he commanded his sons to keep the way of the Lord: to perform righteousness and justice. Therefore, it does not say, “And the loving-kindness,” but “the loving- kindness.” The truth - that you will make come true the promise to Jacob - that will be the payment of the reward for Abraham’s loving-kindness.


###### Rashi on Micah 7:20:3

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/7:20:3](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rashi%20on%20Micah/r/7:20:3)


**אֲשֶׁר נִשְׁבַּעְתָּ.** בַּעֲקֵדָתוֹ שֶׁל יִצְחָק ״בִּי נִשְׁבַּעְתִּי נְאֻם ה׳ יַעַן אֲשֶׁר עָשִׂיתָ וְגוֹמֵר״.


**which you swore**—at the binding of Isaac, (Gen 22:16) “I swore by Myself, says the Lord, that because you did this thing, etc.”
