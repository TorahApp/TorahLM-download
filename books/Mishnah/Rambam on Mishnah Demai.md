# Rambam on Mishnah Demai

רמב"ם על משנה דמאי

Link: [https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rambam%20on%20Mishnah%20Demai](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Rambam%20on%20Mishnah%20Demai)

Maimonides’ first published work, which he began writing in 1161 at the age of 23, including discussions on the Mishnah and legal decisions.
