# Shalom Aleichem

שלום עליכם

Link: [https://torahapp.org/share/book/Shalom%20Aleichem](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Shalom%20Aleichem)

מזמור מסורתי המקבל את פני מלאכי השרת, נהוג אותו לשיר בתחילת סעודת ליל השבת.

Traditional song welcoming ministering angels into the home, commonly sung at the beginning of the Shabbat meal on Friday night.

Sections: Section-Line

Location: Liturgy/Piyutim

## Section 1

###### Shalom Aleichem 1:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Shalom%20Aleichem/r/1:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Shalom%20Aleichem/r/1:1)


שָׁלוֹם עֲלֵיכֶם מַלְאֲכֵי הַשָּׁרֵת, מַלְאֲכֵי עֶלְיוֹן מִמֶּֽלֶךְ מַלְכֵי הַמְּלָכִים, הַקָּדוֹשׁ בָּרוּךְ הוּא.


Peace be with you, ministering angels, messengers of the Most High, sent by the King of Kings, the Holy One, Blessed be He.


###### Shalom Aleichem 1:2

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Shalom%20Aleichem/r/1:2](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Shalom%20Aleichem/r/1:2)


בּוֹאֲכֶם לְשָׁלוֹם מַלְאֲכֵי הַשָּׁלוֹם, מַלְאֲכֵי עֶלְיוֹן מִמֶּֽלֶךְ מַלְכֵי הַמְּלָכִים, הַקָּדוֹשׁ בָּרוּךְ הוּא.


Come in peace, messengers of peace, messengers of the Most High, sent by the King of Kings, the Holy One, Blessed be He.


###### Shalom Aleichem 1:3

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Shalom%20Aleichem/r/1:3](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Shalom%20Aleichem/r/1:3)


בָּרְכֽוּנִי לְשָׁלוֹם מַלְאֲכֵי הַשָּׁלוֹם, מַלְאֲכֵי עֶלְיוֹן מִמֶּֽלֶךְ מַלְכֵי הַמְּלָכִים, הַקָּדוֹשׁ בָּרוּךְ הוּא.


Bless me with peace, messengers of peace, messengers of the Most High, sent by the King of Kings, the Holy One, Blessed be He.


###### Shalom Aleichem 1:4

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Shalom%20Aleichem/r/1:4](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Shalom%20Aleichem/r/1:4)


צֵאתְכֶם לְשָׁלוֹם מַלְאֲכֵי הַשָּׁלוֹם, מַלְאֲכֵי עֶלְיוֹן מִמֶּֽלֶךְ מַלְכֵי הַמְּלָכִים, הַקָּדוֹשׁ בָּרוּךְ הוּא.


Go in peace, messengers of peace, messengers of the Most High, sent by the King of Kings, the Holy One, Blessed be He.
