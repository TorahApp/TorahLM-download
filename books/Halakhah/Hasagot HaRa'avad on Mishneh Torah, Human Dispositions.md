## Chapter 3


### Halakhah 2

###### Hasagot HaRa'avad on Mishneh Torah, Human Dispositions 3:2:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Hasagot%20HaRa%27avad%20on%20Mishneh%20Torah%2C%20Human%20Dispositions/r/3:2:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Hasagot%20HaRa%27avad%20on%20Mishneh%20Torah%2C%20Human%20Dispositions/r/3:2:1)


**וכן כשיבעול וכו'.** א"א וכן למצות העונה אם היא מקפדת:


**Likewise in connubial etc.** Even so in complying with the commandment of a wife's conjugal rights.¹

footnotes:
¹ Herein are outlines of rules for voluntary conduct. Mandatory laws are given, in this instance, in the book dealing with the subject. See Women, Marriage, 14.7. C.G.



## Chapter 7


### Halakhah 2

###### Hasagot HaRa'avad on Mishneh Torah, Human Dispositions 7:2:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Hasagot%20HaRa%27avad%20on%20Mishneh%20Torah%2C%20Human%20Dispositions/r/7:2:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Hasagot%20HaRa%27avad%20on%20Mishneh%20Torah%2C%20Human%20Dispositions/r/7:2:1)


**אבל בעל לשון הרע שיושב ואומר כך עשה איש פלוני.** א"א לא אלא קשה הראשון מן השני שהראשון הוא תליתאי והורג נפשות והשני תנין ואינו הורג אלא את עצמו. בינה זאת:


**The owner of an evil tongue etc.** No, but the first mentioned, namely, the sycophant, is worse than the second, namely, the evil tongue; for the sycophant, mentioned first, is the third to be killed, since sycophancy kills three, whereas the second mentioned is but a sneak killing none but himself.¹

footnotes:
¹ Maimonides maintains that the evil tongue rather than the sycophant kills three. C.
