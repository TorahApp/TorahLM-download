## Halakhah 1

###### Mekorei HaRambam LeRashash on Mishneh Torah, Transmission of the Oral Law 1:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Mekorei%20HaRambam%20LeRashash%20on%20Mishneh%20Torah%2C%20Transmission%20of%20the%20Oral%20Law/r/1:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Mekorei%20HaRambam%20LeRashash%20on%20Mishneh%20Torah%2C%20Transmission%20of%20the%20Oral%20Law/r/1:1)


**תורה זו תורה שבכתב. והמצוה זו פירושה.** בברכות (ה') תורה זו מקרא. והמצוה זו משנה וכו':


"The Torah" refers to the written Torah, and "the mitzvah" refers to its interpretation. In Berakhot (5), "The Torah" refers to the Mikra (Scripture), and "the mitzvah" refers to the Mishnah, etc.

**ונתן ספר לכל שבט ושבט כו'.** כ"ה במדרש רבה סוף פרשת וילך:


"And He gave a Sefer (Torah scroll) to each tribe." This is stated in the Midrash Rabah, at the end of Parashat Vayelech.



## Halakhah 19

###### Mekorei HaRambam LeRashash on Mishneh Torah, Transmission of the Oral Law 19:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Mekorei%20HaRambam%20LeRashash%20on%20Mishneh%20Torah%2C%20Transmission%20of%20the%20Oral%20Law/r/19:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Mekorei%20HaRambam%20LeRashash%20on%20Mishneh%20Torah%2C%20Transmission%20of%20the%20Oral%20Law/r/19:1)


**ומכלל החכמים שקיבלו מרבא רב אשי ורבינא.** תימא דבקדושין (ע"ב:) איתא כשמת רבא נולד רב אשי, ועי' בסה"ד בסדר תנאים ואמוראים ברב אשי:


"And among the sages who received from Rava are Rav Ashi and Ravina. This is surprising, for in Kiddushin (72b) it is written that when Rava passed away, Rav Ashi was born. And see in the *Seder HaDorot*, in the section on the Tanaim and Amoraim, about Rav Ashi."
