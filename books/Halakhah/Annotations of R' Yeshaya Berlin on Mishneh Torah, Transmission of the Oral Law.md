## Halakhah 20

###### Annotations of R' Yeshaya Berlin on Mishneh Torah, Transmission of the Oral Law 20:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Annotations%20of%20R%27%20Yeshaya%20Berlin%20on%20Mishneh%20Torah%2C%20Transmission%20of%20the%20Oral%20Law/r/20:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Annotations%20of%20R%27%20Yeshaya%20Berlin%20on%20Mishneh%20Torah%2C%20Transmission%20of%20the%20Oral%20Law/r/20:1)


**בהקדמת** הרמב"ם ד"ה **נמצא מרב אשי עד משה רבינו ע"ה ארבעים דורות וכו'.** לכאורה פליאה עצומה דבפרטן אי אתה מוצא אלא שלשים ושמונה דורות מן רב אשי עד משה רבינו ע"ה: (בדפוס הזה כבר נתקן בפנים וסרה הפליאה).


"In the introduction of the Rambam, at the beginning of 'It is concluded that from Rav Ashi to Moshe Rabbeinu, may peace be upon him, there are forty generations, etc.' At first glance, this is a great issue, because when counting individually, there are only thirty-eight generations from Rav Ashi to Moshe Rabbeinu, may peace be upon him. (In this edition, this has already been corrected in the text, and the difficulty has been resolved)."


###### Annotations of R' Yeshaya Berlin on Mishneh Torah, Transmission of the Oral Law 20:2

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Annotations%20of%20R%27%20Yeshaya%20Berlin%20on%20Mishneh%20Torah%2C%20Transmission%20of%20the%20Oral%20Law/r/20:2](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Annotations%20of%20R%27%20Yeshaya%20Berlin%20on%20Mishneh%20Torah%2C%20Transmission%20of%20the%20Oral%20Law/r/20:2)


**ואלו הן רב אשי מרבא.** לכאורה תמוה מהא דאיתא קדושין ע"ב. כשמת רבא נולד רב אשי. ושמעתי שגם הרב החסיד מוה' שמשון זצ"ל בעל נזירות שמשון עמד על זה. ואולי תרי רב אשי הוו:


"And these are: Rav Ashi of Rava. At first glance, this is surprising, because of what is written in Kiddushin 72: when Rava passed away, Rav Ashi was born. And I have heard that also the chassidic rabbi, our master Shimshon, of blessed memory, author of *Nezirut Shimshon*, raised this issue. Perhaps there were two Rav Ashis."
