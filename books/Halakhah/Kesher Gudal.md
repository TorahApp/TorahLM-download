## Siman 1

###### Kesher Gudal 1:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kesher%20Gudal/r/1:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kesher%20Gudal/r/1:1)


**סימן א. לקום באשמורת. כל שהיא תפארת.**
א. מיד כשנעור יאמר מודה אני לפניך מלך רחום שהחזרת בי נשמתי בחמלה רבה אמונתך יה״ר שיהא לבי נכון ומסור בידי ולא אכעוס ולא אכעיסך וא״צ נט״י וי״א שצריך (סידור ה׳ יעב״ץ ד״מ):


SIMAN ALEF. TO AWAKEN AT THE MORNING WATCH. As soon as you wake up, say "Mode Ani Lefanecha melech rahum..." and you do not need to wash your hands, and some say that you do need to. (Siddur Hashem of Rabbi Ya`aqov Emden)

ב. מיד יטול ידיו כראוי ואע״פ שאינו רוצה לקום יטול ידיו ברכ״י סי׳ א׳ ולא יטול ממשרתת גויה ולא מישראלית נדה כי הוא בא לדחות הסט״א ודבקתו הרעה (אחרונים):


One must immediately wash his hands properly (according to Jewish law), even if he is not getting up. One should not have a gentile maid, or a Jewess who is a "Nidda", because he can bring on the sitra ahra onto him, and bring bad upon him. (Aharonim)

ג. ילבוש כראוי וטוב שיקום מעומד וקימת חצות מכניע יצ״הר (שם):


One should dress properly and awake and midnight, and one who does can defeat his bad inclination (ibid.)

ד. לא ילך ד׳ אמות בלי נט״י והוא דבר חמור הרבה מאד ואם כלי הרחיצה הם רחוקים ילך פחות פחות מד״א (שם):


One may not walk four Amot (cubits) without washed hands, this is a very strict matter. If he has no cup near him, he should walk less then four cubits (amot). (ibid.) {not like many who rely on the opinion "the whole house is like four cubits"}

It is is forbidden to recite blessings (or learn Torah) in front of the water used to do Netilat Yadayim, one should pour it out or cover it. (ibid.)

(After Asher Yatzar) Right away he should recite all the morning blessings until "VaAni Abarchem" (The end of Brikat HaTorah) (AriZ"al)

(I have said that "Right away recite all the blessings...") And this is after he is already dressed properly, (Because) one should not dress and bless (simultaneously), because one may not do other things while reciting blessings.

In the night, one should say Vidui (The repentance prayer) and the Tiqqun Hazot (The midnight rite) according to the formula of the Ari Za"l - and the time of midnight is 12 hours after midday (See Birke Yosef, Siman Alef).

(After Tiqqun Hazot,) if one is not able to stay awake until the light of the day, he should sleep, and should not remove his clothing - in order not to enter a doubt wether or not to recite the Birkot HaShahar again (See Birke Yosef, Siman Mem Zayin).

One is able to drink coffee without sugar before sunrise, and even after sunrise before prayer. (See Birke Yosef, Siman Pe Tet)



## Siman 2

###### Kesher Gudal 2:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kesher%20Gudal/r/2:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kesher%20Gudal/r/2:1)


**סימן ב. דיני ציצית הנצרכים. ירדו כרוכים:**
א. שיעור הטלית שיקיף הראש וגוף האדם פנים ואחור עד החזה. בר״י סי׳ ט״ז:


SIMAN BET. THE LAWS OF SISITH. The required size of the Tallit: It must be large enough to cover the face and body until the chest. (Birke Yosef Siman Tet Zayin)


###### Kesher Gudal 2:2

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kesher%20Gudal/r/2:2](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kesher%20Gudal/r/2:2)


ב. ילבש טלית קטן על החלוק ויישן עמו ולא יסירנו כ״א במרחץ. שם סי׳ ח׳:


The Tallit Katan is worn on a garment, and is meant to be slept with, and not removed in a bathroom. (Birke Yosef Siman Het)


###### Kesher Gudal 2:3

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kesher%20Gudal/r/2:3](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kesher%20Gudal/r/2:3)


ג. יפריד הציציות תמיד ואם מסובכין קצת אסור להפרידם בשבת. גן המלך סימן ס״ה:


The strings (of the sisit) should always be separated. If they were a little stuck together, one may not separated them on Shabbat. (Gan HaMelech Siman Samech He)


###### Kesher Gudal 2:4

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kesher%20Gudal/r/2:4](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kesher%20Gudal/r/2:4)


ד. יברך להתעטף בציצית הבי״ת בניקוד שו״א. ברכ״י סימן ח׳:


When blessing "Lehit'atef B'sisit" with a Sheva under the Bet (B'sisit not Basisit) (Birke Yosef Siman Het).


###### Kesher Gudal 2:5

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kesher%20Gudal/r/2:5](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kesher%20Gudal/r/2:5)


ה. אחר שבירך יתעטף בטלית. אחרונים דלא כבית יעקב ויהיה הטלית בידו קודם ברכה. מחב״ר סי׳ ח׳:


After blessing do the attifa {wrapping the Tallit around the head like an Arab, '''leaving the face uncovered'''', and first wrapping the left corners on the right shoulder, than on top of those corners {still on the right shoulder} put the right corners}. The Aharonim don't rule like the Bet Yaaqob (who rules to cover the face). (Shulhan Aruch Se'if Het)

ו. עטיפת ציצית מעומד ויברך מעומד:


The wrapping of the tallit takes place standing.


###### Kesher Gudal 2:7

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kesher%20Gudal/r/2:7](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kesher%20Gudal/r/2:7)


ז. צריך להסתכל בציצית ולחשוב כאלו יש בו תכלת דאותה הארה לא נפסקה. בר״י סי׳ ח׳:


There is an obligation on everyone to look at the sisit strings and to imagine he is wearing it with techelet (a blue string). (Birke Yosef Siman Het)


###### Kesher Gudal 2:8

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kesher%20Gudal/r/2:8](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kesher%20Gudal/r/2:8)

On the topic of covering the head with the Tallit, it is clear and obvious, that when saying the blessing he must wrap his head like an Arab (as said above) for the time it takes to walk four amot (about two seconds), and afterward, he can wear it according to his minhag. (Mahaziq Berach Siman Het)


###### Kesher Gudal 2:9

The holes [in the corners of the garment] that hold the sisit, may not be more than three finger breadths [about the size of an adults thumb-nail] away from the edges of the corner. (See Mahaziq Bracha Siman Yud Alef)

י. חוט א׳ שנפסק כלו פסול ואם נפסק חוט מארבעה במקום שתלוי׳ בנקב פסול. שם סי׳ י״ב:


One entire string that rips off deems the whole invalid. And if a string rips off [not entirely, but-] at the knot near the whole, deems it invalid. (ibid. Siman Yud Bet)


###### Kesher Gudal 2:11

One who didn't have sisit or tefillin, [and finds some] in the middle of pesuqe deZimra, may make a blessing in the middle of the psalms. (see Birke Yosef Siman Nun Gimel). But not at the end of pesuqe diZimra before Yishtabach, but rather (he should wait till after barechu) between Yishtabach and the Beracha Yosser Or. (Shulhan Aruch ibid.)


###### Kesher Gudal 2:12

One who sleeps with his tallit at night isn't required to make another blessing in the morning (Acharonim in siman Het) {Although the Shulhan Aruch rules to make a blessing, it is a case of Safeq berachot (a doubt whether or not to say a blessing), and maybe even a Safeq Safeq Safeqah (triple doubt), and we can apply the ruling "Safeq Berachot LeHaQel, afilu neghed Maran" - We rule lenient (not to recite) blessings (in a time of doubt) , even if it is not like the opinion of Maran (the Shulhan Aruch)}

One may not drag the sisit strings on the floor, and if the tallit is too long, he should take the two back sisiyot and tuck it into his belt. (Birke Yosef Siman Kaf Alef)



## Siman 3

###### Kesher Gudal 3:1

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kesher%20Gudal/r/3:1](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kesher%20Gudal/r/3:1)


**סימן ג. קצת דיני תפילין. החלו עולין:**
א. ילבש טלית גדול ואח״כ ילבש תפילין הגם שטלית קטן כבר הוא עליו תמיד. בר״י סימן כ״ה:


SIMAN GIMMEL. LAWS OF TEFILLIN.: He dons in the Tallit Gadol, and afterwards, he dons Tefillin, and this is even though he has a Tallit Qattan already on him always. (Birke Yosef Siman Kaf He)


###### Kesher Gudal 3:2

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kesher%20Gudal/r/3:2](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kesher%20Gudal/r/3:2)


ב. ההולך בדרך יזהר ללבוש תפילין דיד וראש ולא יבטל מצוה גדולה וחמורה מפני לעג השאננים אך יעשה כל הבא בידו שיהיה בצנעא ויש מי שכתב דיניח של יד לבד. שם סימן כ״ו:


When walking on his way (to synagogue) a man should be careful to wear Tefillin, both the hand Tefillin and the head Tefillin, and not to be negligent this great and strict Missva because of mockery from others, and from this he he''l be humble in all his ways. Many people only put shel yad Siman Kaf Vav


###### Kesher Gudal 3:3

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kesher%20Gudal/r/3:3](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kesher%20Gudal/r/3:3)


ג. אם שכח ולבש תפילה של ראש קודם של יד א״צ להסירה אלא ילבש של יד. שם ושיורי ברכה:


If one forgets and puts on the Tefila Shel Rosh before the Shel Yad, one doesn't need to remove it, rather he should put on the Tefila Shel Yad then, (without removing the Shel Rosh first). Shiurei Beracha ibid.


###### Kesher Gudal 3:4

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kesher%20Gudal/r/3:4](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kesher%20Gudal/r/3:4)


ד. וישתדל שבכיס התפילין יניח של ראש ואח״כ של יד כדי שיפגע בשל יד והנכון שהכיס יהיה רחב שיהיה של ראש ושל יד פנים בפנים אך של יד תהיה יותר מיומנת ומזומנת. בר״י סי׳ כ״ח:


One should be careful when putting away the Tefillin to put the Shel Rosh and then the Shel Yad (should be in front of the Shel Rosh) in order to take out the Shel Yad from muscle memory (not to by mistake take out the Shel Rosh first). It is even more correct to make the Tefillin pouch wide (and instead of putting the Shel Yad in front of the Shel Rosh) to put it side by side, (but to still make sure that) the Shel Yad should be in the side which the right hand is accustomed to reach for {for example: if one's right hand normally reaches for the left side of the bag, he should put the Shel Yad on the left}. (Birke Yosef Siman Kaf Het)


###### Kesher Gudal 3:5

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kesher%20Gudal/r/3:5](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kesher%20Gudal/r/3:5)


ה. כשמניח תפילין של יד על הקבורת מקום הנחתן יברך ואח״כ יהדקם ש״ע סי׳ כ״ה:


(The correct sequence is:) to put the Tefillin on the bicep in the correct place, then to bless the blessing (leHaniach Tefillin) and only then, to tighten the knot on hid arm (Shulhan Aruch Siman Kaf He)


###### Kesher Gudal 3:6

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kesher%20Gudal/r/3:6](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kesher%20Gudal/r/3:6)


ו. אסור להפסיק בדבור בין של יד לשל ראש ואם הפסיק מברך על של ראש על מצות תפילין. שם:


It is forbidden to interrupt [With speach] between putting on the Shel Yad and putting on the Shel Rosh. If one does interrupt, he must also make a blessing on the Shel Rosh "`Al Misswath Tefillin". (ibid.) {and not like the Ashkenazim who always make a blessing on the Shel Rosh.


###### Kesher Gudal 3:7

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kesher%20Gudal/r/3:7](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kesher%20Gudal/r/3:7)


ז. אחר שמהדק תפלה של יד יקשור היוד עם התפלה וממשיך הרצועה אצל הזרוע אבל בקבורת לא יעשה שום כריכה. בר״י סי׳ כ״ה ושיורי ברכה שם:


After tightening the Shel Yad (onto his arm), he must (wrap the strap around the box in order to) tie the "Yud" knot with the box, and after that he must continue and wrap the strap on the arms (seven or eight times depending on the custom), but one may not wrap the strap even once on the bicep, {unlike many Sepharadim and Ashkenazim who wrap once one the arm before wrapping on the forearm once, and surely not like those "Hasidim" who wrap three or six times before wrapping on the forearm, to make a Shin}


###### Kesher Gudal 3:8

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kesher%20Gudal/r/3:8](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kesher%20Gudal/r/3:8)


ח. אחר שיכרוך ז׳ כריכות סביב לזרוע יניח של ראש בר״י שם:


After wrapping seven times on the arm {or eight, depending the custom, [whether or not to count the first wrap as a real wrap, since it is only a half wrap]} he must place the Shel Rosh (Birke Yosef ibid.) {And not like those who wrap first on the hand, then put the Shel Rosh, then unwrap the hand, and then wrap the finger and etc.}


###### Kesher Gudal 3:9

One may not take out the Shel Rosh from the pouch before putting on the Shel Yad. (Shulhan Arukh ibid.)

י. יניח קציצה של ראש מיושבת ומושכבת ומהתחלת עיקרי שער הפדחת יניח התיתורא. שיורי ברכה סי׳ ז״ך:


###### Kesher Gudal 3:11

[https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kesher%20Gudal/r/3:11](https://torahapp.org/share/book/Kesher%20Gudal/r/3:11)


יא. השחור שברצועות יהיה לצד חוץ ולא יתהפכו. ש״ע סי׳ ז״ך. ויש מי שכתב שישחירם מבית ומחוץ:
